Laâyoune

The capital of the Moroccan Sahara, wide grand avenues, golden coastal dunes, and the Atlantic Ocean.

Best Time to Visit Oct – Apr
Ideal Stay 2 – 3 Days
Top Vibe Desert & Atlantic Coast
Nearest Airport EUN (Hassan I)

Welcome to Laâyoune — Where the Desert Meets the Atlantic

Laâyoune is the largest city and economic heart of the Moroccan Sahara. Situated on the dry bed of the Saguia el-Hamra river and just 25 kilometers from the Atlantic shore, it bridges modern urban development with centuries-old nomadic customs.

From traditional three-round Sahrawi tea ceremonies to fresh seafood catches at the port of El Marsa and peaceful ocean sunsets at Foum El Oued beach, Laâyoune offers an authentic southern desert experience.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A balanced plan to discover the city landmarks, the port, and the surrounding desert.

Morning — Place Mechouar & Great Mosque

Stroll around Place Mechouar, admiring the Sahrawi architectural motifs and broad palm-lined boulevards.

Afternoon — Souk Soune & Artisanal Crafts

Browse traditional silver jewelry, camel-leather goods, and colorful melhfa fabrics in local markets.

Evening — Traditional Sahrawi Tea Ceremony

Experience local tea hospitality (Atay) served in three distinct rounds in a relaxed city café.

Morning — El Marsa Fishing Port

Head west to the port town of El Marsa to observe fishing boats returning with the daily Atlantic catch.

Afternoon — Foum El Oued Ocean Relax

Enjoy lunch with freshly grilled fish, then unwind along the wide sandy beach of Foum El Oued.

Evening — Sunset over Coastal Dunes

Watch the sunset as the golden desert dunes merge directly into the ocean shore.

Morning — Saguia el-Hamra Valley Excursion

Explore the palm groves and dry river bed landscapes north-east of Laâyoune.

Afternoon — Camel Ride & Dune Trek

Take a short camel trek across the golden dunes surrounding the city limits.

Evening — Farewell Sahrawi Dinner

Enjoy camel meat tagine or fresh grilled sea bass in one of Laâyoune's authentic local restaurants.

Practical Tips for Visiting Laâyoune

Getting Around

Yellow petit taxis are plentiful and economical within the city. Grand taxis run frequently to El Marsa and Foum El Oued beach.

Weather & Climate

Enjoyable sunny desert climate year-round with mild Atlantic ocean breezes. Evening temperatures can drop noticeably in winter.

Sahrawi Etiquette

Hospitality is deep-rooted. Accepting tea when offered is customary. Respect local social norms and ask before photographing people.

Good to Know

Laâyoune Travel FAQ

Is Laâyoune easy to reach?

Yes, Hassan I Airport (EUN) offers regular direct domestic flights from Casablanca and Agadir, as well as international connections. The N1 coastal highway connects it smoothly by road.

How far is the beach from the city?

Foum El Oued beach and the port of El Marsa are located about 20 to 25 kilometers west of the city center, reachable in 20 minutes by taxi or car.

How many days should I spend in Laâyoune?

2 to 3 days allow you to explore the city squares, enjoy fresh seafood at the port, relax on Foum El Oued beach, and take a dune excursion.

What is the best month to visit Laâyoune?

From October to April, the climate is pleasantly warm and sunny, avoiding the intense heat of peak summer.
