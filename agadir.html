Agadir

Wide Atlantic bay, modern corniche, hilltop Kasbah views and a gateway to Taghazout surf and Paradise Valley — Agadir is Morocco’s premier beach capital.

Best Time to Visit Apr – Jun & Sep – Nov
Ideal Stay 3 – 5 Days
Top Vibe Sunny & Beach
Nearest Airport AGA (Al Massira)

Welcome to Morocco’s Atlantic Beach Capital

Agadir is Morocco’s big Atlantic beach city — rebuilt after the 1960 earthquake into a wide, modern resort destination rather than a traditional medina maze. What you get instead is a long sandy bay, a palm-lined corniche, and a hilltop Kasbah that still watches over everything. The pace is relaxed, sun-drenched, and built for days that end with salt on your skin and fresh seafood on the plate.

Climb the Kasbah at golden hour for the classic curve of the bay. Lose a morning in Souk El Had, one of the largest vibrant markets in the country. Use the city as a base for Taghazout’s surf breaks, Paradise Valley’s palm pools, and excursions into the Souss region.

Top Sightseeing

Must-Visit Places in Agadir

Beach, Kasbah, souk, marina and the landmarks that define the city.

Beach

Agadir Beach

Long Atlantic sand arc backed by resorts and the corniche — swim, walk and watch the bay at sunset.

Viewpoint

Kasbah (Agadir Oufella)

Hilltop historic ruins with the classic panoramic view over the entire bay and modern port.

Market

Souk El Had

One of Morocco’s largest walled markets — spices, argan oil, crafts, textiles and daily local life.

Marina

Agadir Marina

Modern upscale marina with yachts, waterfront dining, cafés and a peaceful promenade.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A balanced schedule to experience the highlights of Agadir and its surroundings.

Morning — Kasbah Oufella Viewpoint

Head up to the historic Kasbah ruins for sweeping views over the Atlantic bay. Go early for soft morning light.

Afternoon — Souk El Had

Explore the bustling stalls of Souk El Had for spices, argan oil, leather goods and local souvenirs.

Evening — Corniche Sunset Walk

Stroll along the lively seaside promenade as the sun sets over the ocean.

Morning — Agadir Beach & Watersports

Enjoy the wide sandy beach, camel rides by the shore, or a surf lesson.

Afternoon — Agadir Marina & Port

Walk around the marina boutiques and enjoy fresh grilled fish at the nearby fishing port.

Evening — Dinner by the Bay

Relax at a seaside restaurant along the corniche with traditional Moroccan cuisine.

Morning — Day Trip to Taghazout

Head 30 minutes north to the famous surf village of Taghazout for bohemian cafés and ocean waves.

Afternoon — Paradise Valley Pools

Visit the natural palm gorges and rock pools in the Atlas foothills for a refreshing afternoon.

Evening — Return to Agadir

Final evening in the city centre or relaxing at your resort.

Practical Tips for Visiting Agadir

Getting Around

Petits taxis operate inside the city (ensure the meter is on); grands taxis are available for trips to Taghazout or Paradise Valley.

Weather & Clothing

Agadir enjoys over 300 days of sunshine, but evenings by the Atlantic breeze can be cool. Always pack a light jacket.

Safety & Vibe

Agadir is one of Morocco's safest and most relaxed resort cities for international travelers and families.

Good to Know

Agadir Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Agadir?

3 to 4 days give you enough time to enjoy the beach, explore Souk El Had, visit the Kasbah, and do day trips to Taghazout or Paradise Valley.

How do I get from Al Massira Airport to the city centre?

Official airport taxis are available right outside arrivals (fixed rate to the city centre is around 200–250 MAD). Private hotel transfers are also very common.

Is Agadir safe for tourists?

Yes, Agadir is very safe. The tourist zones, beach promenade, and marina are heavily policed and welcoming.
