Andalusian Gardens

Shaded paths and fountains beside the Kasbah of the Udayas.

Best Time to Go Midday shade after the kasbah
Time Needed 30 – 60 minutes
Entry Often modest ticket
Where Beside Kasbah of the Udayas

A green pause under the kasbah walls

The Andalusian Gardens sit next to the Kasbah of the Udayas — formal paths, citrus and shade that offer a break from white walls and Atlantic glare.

It is not a vast botanical destination; it is a designed garden that completes a kasbah morning. Families and photographers use it for softer light and quieter benches.

Visit after the kasbah lanes or before the river viewpoint. Ticket rules can differ from the free residential streets — check at the entrance.

Practical Tips for Visiting the Andalusian Gardens

Treat it as the kasbah’s cooler room

Do the bright lanes and viewpoint first or second, and use the garden when you need shade. It is small enough that a slow loop is better than racing every path for photos.

Tickets and opening rhythm

Confirm fee and closing time at the entrance — garden hours can differ from the always-open residential streets of the kasbah. Last entry matters if you arrive late afternoon.

Photography without blocking paths

Popular corners fill with posed shoots. Step aside, keep paths clear, and save drone ideas for elsewhere — this is a compact public garden beside homes.

Good to Know

Andalusian Gardens FAQ

Is there a ticket?

Often a modest fee separate from walking the kasbah streets — confirm at the gate.

How long do I need?

Thirty to sixty minutes is enough for most visits.

Good with children?

Yes — open paths and shade; still supervise near any water features.

Best time?

Late morning after the kasbah, or midday when you need shade most.

Can I picnic?

Follow posted rules; many visitors simply sit on benches with water, not full picnics.
