Mount Gourougou

A low mountain above Nador — trails and wide views over lagoon, coast and Melilla on clear days.

Best time Morning for cooler air
Suggested duration Half day
Entry Free trails; transfers extra
Location Above Nador

Height over the lagoon city

Mount Gourougou (Gurugu) rises beside Nador and Melilla, offering short hikes and road viewpoints that put the lagoon and Mediterranean coast in one frame.

On clear days the view can stretch toward Melilla and the wider shoreline. Trails vary in difficulty; sturdy shoes and water are essential in warm months.

Go early to avoid heat. Combine with a relaxed corniche evening rather than stacking another long transfer the same day.

Food near Mount Gourougou

Plan meals in town, on the corniche, or pack a simple picnic.

  • Bottled water on every outing.
  • Fish and simple plates in town.
  • Corniche cafés in the evening.
  • Confirm hours before you go.
  • Cash is useful outside larger hotels.

Carry dirhams for taxis and small stops.

Good to know

Mount Gourougou FAQ

Do I need a guide?

Not for every viewpoint; useful for longer trail routes.

How hard is it?

Options range from easy viewpoints to steeper hikes — match the route to your fitness.

Best season?

Spring and autumn for temperature and visibility.

Can I drive up?

Some viewpoints are road-accessible; confirm current access locally.

How long?

A half day covers most visitor routes including transfer.
