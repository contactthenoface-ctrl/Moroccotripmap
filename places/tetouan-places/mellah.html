The Mellah of Tetouan

The historic Jewish quarter of Tetouan, reflecting a unique blend of Andalusian heritage, distinctive architecture, and vibrant cultural roots.

Best Time to Go Morning or late afternoon
Time Needed 45 – 60 minutes
Entry Free (public access)
Where Old Town, Tetouan

A historical quarter with distinct architecture

Established in the 19th century outside the old Andalusian medina walls, the Mellah of Tetouan is an essential part of the city's diverse cultural fabric. It reflects the deep ties between the local Jewish community and the Spanish-Andalusian history of northern Morocco.

Unlike the winding alleys of the main medina, the Mellah features wider streets and houses with unique European-influenced windows and balconies overlooking the public spaces.

Walking through its quiet lanes offers a profound glimpse into Tetouan's multi-layered heritage, complementing your exploration of the nearby Royal Palace and historic souks.

Practical Tips for Visiting the Mellah

Combine with the Royal Palace

The Mellah is located very close to Place Hassan II and the Royal Palace, making it easy to combine them in a single walk.

Respect local residents

The Mellah is a residential neighborhood. Be respectful of private homes and residents while taking photos.

Wear comfortable shoes

As with most historic areas in Tetouan, walking is the best way to explore the neighborhood.

Good to Know

Tetouan Mellah FAQ

Is the Mellah free to visit?

Yes. Walking through the streets and alleys of the Mellah is entirely free and open to everyone.

How long do I need to explore?

45 to 60 minutes is usually enough for a relaxed walk through the quarter.

Can I visit without a guide?

Yes, it is very easy to explore independently, especially since it connects smoothly with Place Hassan II.
