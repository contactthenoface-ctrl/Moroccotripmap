Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When's the best time to go?

Honestly, spring and autumn win almost every time — March to May or September to November, warm enough for the desert without being brutal in the cities. Summer works fine on the coast; it's really the interior and the south that get punishing.

Will I need a visa?

If you're coming from the EU, UK, US, Canada or most of Latin America, no — you get 90 days visa-free on arrival. Rules do shift though, so it's worth a quick check for your passport before booking flights.

How long should I actually spend in Marrakech?

Three or four full days is the sweet spot — enough for the medina, a palace or two, and a slower morning without feeling like you're racing the clock. Add a day trip into the Atlas or the Agafay desert if you've got the time.

Is it safe to explore on your own?

Yes — most people travel around Morocco independently without any issues. Use the same common sense you would in any busy city: keep an eye on your bag in the souks, and settle the taxi fare before you get in, not after.
