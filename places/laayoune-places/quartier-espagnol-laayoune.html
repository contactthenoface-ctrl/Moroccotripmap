The First Nucleus of the City

The Quartier Espagnol (Spanish Quarter) is the historic core of Laâyoune. During the Spanish colonial period, military and residential structures were built here, forming the first organised urban nucleus of the city on the edge of the Saguia el-Hamra.

Characteristic features include the distinctive semi-circular or dome-like houses known locally as « leqbibat », a relatively orthogonal street plan, and the quiet atmosphere of a neighbourhood that has preserved much of its early character. Nearby stands the Cathedral of Saint Francis of Assisi, another remnant of the Spanish era.

Walking these streets offers a different face of Laâyoune – quieter, more intimate, and rich in the architectural memory of the city’s colonial beginnings, now fully integrated into Moroccan Saharan life.

Practical Tips for Visiting the Quartier Espagnol

How to Explore

Best discovered on foot. Start from the central area or Place Mechouar and walk into the older grid of streets. A local guide can point out the most characteristic houses and the cathedral.

Respect Residential Life

These are lived-in streets. Be discreet with photography of people and private homes, and keep noise low.

Combine with Nearby Sights

The Spanish Quarter pairs naturally with Place Mechouar, the cathedral area, the central markets and a short visit to the Mosquée Moulay Abdellah.

Good to Know

Quartier Espagnol FAQ

Is the Spanish Quarter free to visit?

Yes. It is an open residential and historic neighbourhood with no entrance fee.

What is special about the houses?

Many of the older houses have distinctive semi-circular or dome-like forms (locally called leqbibat), designed in the Spanish colonial period and well suited to the climate.

Is there a cathedral?

Yes. The Cathedral of Saint Francis of Assisi, built during the Spanish period, stands near the historic quarter and is one of the notable colonial-era buildings.

How long should I spend there?

1 to 2 hours is ideal for a relaxed walk through the streets and a sense of the atmosphere.

Is it safe and easy to reach?

Yes. It is a normal urban neighbourhood in central Laâyoune, easily reached on foot or by petit taxi from Place Mechouar and the main hotels.
