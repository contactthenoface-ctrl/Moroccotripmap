Nejjarine Museum of Wooden Arts & Crafts

Explore the Nejjarine Museum in Fes, a beautifully restored funduq showcasing Morocco’s traditional woodworking and artisan heritage.

The Nejjarine Museum of Wooden Arts and Crafts occupies a beautifully restored historic funduq in the heart of Fes el-Bali. The building itself is part of the experience, with a finely decorated courtyard and traditional architectural details that reward a slow visit.

Inside, the collection explores Morocco’s woodworking traditions through tools, furniture, architectural pieces and everyday objects. The museum gives useful context to the cedar woodwork seen throughout Fes, especially in madrasas, riads and historic houses.

One of the highlights is the rooftop terrace, where the dense medina unfolds around you. It is a good place to pause after walking the narrow streets and to appreciate how the old city is organized from above.

What to see and how to experience it

Set aside time to explore the area at an unhurried pace. Nejjarine Fountain, Al Quaraouiyine, Fes el-Bali, souks are nearby, making it easy to build this stop into a wider Fes walking route. The most rewarding visits usually leave room for small discoveries rather than rushing from one landmark to another.

Practical Info

  • Address: Place Nejjarine, Fes el-Bali
  • Hours: Opening hours vary; check current museum information
  • Ticket: Admission fee applies
  • Tip: Allow about 45–90 minutes, including the terrace

Hours and prices can change without notice — check official sources before your visit.
