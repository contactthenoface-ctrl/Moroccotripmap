Practical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions for Morocco Travelers

Most car rental agencies and police checkpoints accept national driver's licenses issued in Latin characters (EU, UK, US, Canada). An International Driving Permit is recommended if your license uses non-Latin script.
In urban tourist hubs like Marrakech or Agadir beach resorts, casual summer wear is widely accepted. In traditional medinas, religious monuments, and Atlas mountain villages, modest clothing covering shoulders and knees is appreciated as a sign of respect.
Major hotels, modern restaurants, and official stores accept credit cards. However, cash in Moroccan Dirhams (MAD) is essential for souks, taxis, tipping, small eateries, and when traveling through desert or mountain oases.
