Fint Oasis

Palm oasis and village lanes south of Ouarzazate — greenery beyond the film sets.

Best time Morning or late afternoon
Suggested duration 2 – 3 hours
Entry Village visit — guide optional
Location South of Ouarzazate

Green against the dry hills

Fint Oasis offers palm gardens, irrigation channels and village paths instead of studio façades.

Often arranged with a driver. Respect private gardens and ask before photographing people.

Eat around Fint

Simple hospitality may be offered on tours. Otherwise plan meals in Ouarzazate.

  • Ask your driver about lunch.
  • Carry water.
  • Do not assume restaurants.
  • Support fair local purchases.
  • Dinner back in town.

Landscape and village visit more than a dining destination.

Practical tips

Getting there

Private taxi or excursion (about 30–45 minutes).

Respect

Stay on paths; ask before photos of people.

Footwear

Closed shoes for dusty or uneven paths.

Good to know

FAQ about Fint Oasis

How far from Ouarzazate?

About 30–45 minutes by road.

Is a guide required?

Not mandatory, but helpful.

Good for children?

Yes at an easy pace in the shade.

Same day as Aït Benhaddou?

Possible but full — many prefer a separate half-day.

What makes it special?

Water and palms so close to the dry plateau.
