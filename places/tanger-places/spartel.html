Cap Spartel

Atlantic headland and lighthouse west of Tangier — often paired with Hercules Caves.

Best time Clear mornings
Suggested duration 45 – 90 minutes on site
Entry Outdoor areas generally free
Location West of Tangier (~20–30 min)
Overview

Where the Atlantic meets the strait approaches

Cap Spartel is the rocky Atlantic cape west of Tangier, marked by its lighthouse and open horizons. It is a short stop for views and photos, usually combined with Hercules Caves on the same taxi loop.

Agree a round-trip price and waiting time with a petit taxi in town. Wind is common — secure hats and light layers.

Good to know

FAQ about Cap Spartel

Distance from Tangier?

About 20–30 minutes by road west of the city.

Can I enter the lighthouse?

Access rules vary; the outdoor viewpoint is the main visit for most travellers.

Combine with Hercules Caves?

Yes — the standard half-day outing from Tangier.

Worth it in bad weather?

Wind and fog reduce the experience; clear days are far better.

Entry fee?

Outdoor areas are generally free; any lighthouse or parking fees are local and modest.
