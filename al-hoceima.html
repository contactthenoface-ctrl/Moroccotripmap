Al Hoceima

Where the Rif meets the Mediterranean: cliff-backed coves, Quemado Beach, a national park of sea and mountains, and a calm northern bay.

Best time May – October for beaches; spring & autumn for hiking
Ideal stay 3 – 5 days
Vibe Mediterranean bay, cliffs, relaxed Rif coast
Airport Al Hoceima (AHU)

Welcome to Al Hoceima — bay, cliffs and Mediterranean light

Al Hoceima sits on a wide natural bay on Morocco’s Mediterranean shore, framed by the Rif mountains and the protected coastline of Al Hoceima National Park. The city is low-key; the draw is the water — golden urban beaches, jewel-like coves and boat-only inlets under high limestone cliffs.

Travellers come for Quemado and Sfiha, for Cala Bonita and Cala Iris, for park trails and short boat trips along the cliffs. Three to five days leave room for beach time, a park outing and slow evenings on the corniche.

Practical tips for Al Hoceima

Getting around

Town is compact; beaches are short taxi hops. National park coves often need a car, organised trip or boat. Agree prices clearly.

Sun & sea

Mediterranean sun is strong in summer. High SPF, water and shade for beach days. Check sea conditions before park boat trips.

Cash & seasons

Carry dirhams for taxis and small stops. Peak beach season is summer; spring and autumn are quieter for hiking and views.

Good to know

Al Hoceima travel FAQ

How many days in Al Hoceima?

Three days cover the main beaches and one park outing. Five days suit slower beach time plus Cala Iris, Torres de Alcalá and Bades without rushing.

Is Al Hoceima only for beaches?

Beaches are the main draw, but the national park, cliff walks, port life and Rif hinterland add nature and local rhythm beyond sunbathing.

How do I get to Al Hoceima?

Domestic flights to AHU from major Moroccan cities, or long road links via the Rif from Tetouan, Chefchaouen or Fez corridors.

Best time to visit?

May to October for swimming and beach weather. Spring and autumn are excellent for hiking, views and fewer crowds.
