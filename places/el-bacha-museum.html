Dar El Bacha

A refined early-20th-century palace in the Medina, known for its architecture, courtyards and cultural collections.

About Dar El Bacha

Dar El Bacha is one of those Marrakech buildings where the architecture itself is the reason to slow down. Courtyards, carved details, zellige and painted wood create a more intimate experience than the city's larger palace complexes.

The site is also associated with Marrakech's cultural life and the popular Bacha Coffee experience. Its location makes it an easy addition to a Medina day focused on museums, souks and historic residences.

What to see and how to experience it

  • •Explore the central courtyards and decorative details.
  • •Look closely at the zellige, carved plaster and painted wood.
  • •Take time to appreciate the palace's proportions and quiet corners.
  • •Combine Dar El Bacha with Le Jardin Secret and the northern Medina.

A little context

Dar El Bacha was built in the early 20th century and became associated with Thami El Glaoui, the Pasha of Marrakech.

Practical Information

Location
Medina, Marrakech
Time to allow
1–1.5 hours
Best time
Morning or afternoon
MoroccoTripMap tip

Check current museum and café arrangements before visiting, as access and opening details can change.
