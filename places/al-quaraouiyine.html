Heritage

Al Quaraouiyine

A historic centre of learning and one of the city's most important spiritual landmarks.

Founded in 859 AD by Fatima al-Fihri, Al Quaraouiyine is recognized by UNESCO and the Guinness World Records as the oldest continually operating university in the world. It stands as a pivotal monument of Islamic architecture and higher learning in Fes.

Throughout centuries, it attracted renowned scholars from across the Mediterranean basin, contributing immensely to philosophy, mathematics, medicine, and Islamic jurisprudence.

While entrance to the active prayer halls is reserved for Muslims, non-Muslim visitors can admire the magnificent open courtyard through its main doorways, featuring zellige tilework, marble fountains, and exquisite Andalusian carved wood details.

Practical Info

  • Address: Qayruwan Quarter, Fes el-Bali Medina
  • Access: Courtyard views from main gates for non-Muslims
  • Best time to visit: Morning or early afternoon
  • Getting there: Located in the central heart of the medina, accessible via Tala'a Kebira

Hours and access details can change without notice — check official sources before your visit.
