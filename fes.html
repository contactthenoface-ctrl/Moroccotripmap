Fez

The spiritual capital of Morocco, home to ancestral craftsmanship and guardian of a UNESCO-listed thousand-year-old medina.

Best Time to Visit Mar – May / Sep – Nov
Ideal Stay 2 – 3 Days
Vibe Authentic & Spiritual
Main Airport Fez-Saïss (FEZ)

Welcome to the Soul of Morocco

Fez is not merely a place you visit—it is an immersion into living history. Passing through the iconic blue gate of Bab Boujloud feels like stepping centuries back in time. With over 9,000 winding alleyways, Fez el-Bali forms the largest car-free urban area in the world, where the scent of carved cedarwood blends with exotic spices and freshly tanned leather.

Allow yourself to get lost in its maze. Tucked behind unpretentious alley walls lie the city's greatest treasures: Andalusia-inspired madrasas, the grandeur of Al-Qarawiyyin, and serene palace riads offering peaceful sanctuaries from the vibrant souks outside.

Planning Your Trip

Recommended 3-Day Itinerary

Designed to let you experience the essence of Fez at a comfortable pace without missing key landmarks.

Day 1

Heart of Fez el-Bali

Start at Bab Boujloud gate, descend Talaa Kebira and Talaa Seghira. Observe coppersmiths in Place Seffarine before watching leather dyers at work in Chouara Tannery.

Full Day • Historic Medina
Day 2

Architecture & Jewish Heritage

Marvel at the craftsmanship inside Bou Inania Madrasa. Head to Fez el-Jdid to stroll through El Mellah, visit Ibn Danan Synagogue, and photograph the Royal Palace brass doors.

Full Day • Fez el-Jdid & Mellah
Day 3

Panoramas & Artistry

Visit Fondouk Nejjarine (Wood Museum), sample local pastilla, and take a short taxi up to the Marinid Tombs for a breathtaking panoramic sunset across the ancient cityscape.

Half Day • Heights & Panoramas

A stay of 2 to 3 days is ideal to explore the historic medina (Fez el-Bali), visit key madrasas, see the tanneries, and enjoy panoramic hilltop views. If you wish to take day trips to Meknes, Volubilis, or Moulay Idriss, allow 4 days in total.
Yes, Fez el-Bali features over 9,000 narrow streets, making it easy to lose your bearings. Getting lost is part of the experience! However, main thoroughfares like Talaa Kebira and Talaa Seghira have clear direction signs. Offline navigation apps like Maps.me or Google Maps work very well.
Hiring a licensed official guide for your first full day is highly recommended. A guide will share valuable historical context, navigate complex alleyways efficiently, and help you avoid unofficial touts around the main artisan quarters.
Spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) offer pleasant weather with comfortable daily temperatures between 20°C and 26°C. Summer (July-August) can be intensely hot, exceeding 40°C, while winter nights can be cool.
