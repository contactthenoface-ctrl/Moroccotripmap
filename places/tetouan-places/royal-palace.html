Royal Palace

The majestic royal residence overlooking Place Hassan II — grandeur, striking Hispano-Moorish architecture and royal guards.

Best Time to Go Late afternoon golden hour
Time Needed 30 – 45 minutes
Entry Exterior view only
Where Place Hassan II, Tetouan

Majesty on Place Hassan II

The Royal Palace of Tetouan (*Dar al-Makhzen*), located on the expansive Place Hassan II, stands as a stunning example of Hispano-Moorish architecture. Built and restored across centuries, it serves as the official royal residence in northern Morocco when the King is in town.

While the interior is closed to the public as an active residence, the majestic gates, ornate green-tiled roofs, and the dignified presence of the royal guards draw visitors from all over. It acts as the grand transition point between the modern Ensanche district and the ancient UNESCO-listed medina.

Stroll across the square in the late afternoon to witness local life unfold against this striking historic backdrop.

Practical Tips for Visiting the Royal Palace

Exterior visit only

The palace is an active royal residence; entry is not permitted, but the exterior view and gates offer great photo opportunities.

Best photography light

Visit during late afternoon when the warm sun illuminates the ornate facade and Place Hassan II comes alive.

Gateway to the Medina

The palace sits right next to Bab el-Okla and Bab Tut entrances, making it an ideal starting point for exploring the medina.

Good to Know

Royal Palace FAQ

Can visitors enter the Royal Palace?

No, the interior is an active royal residence and is closed to the public. Visitors can admire and photograph the grand exterior and gates from Place Hassan II.

Is there an admission fee?

No fee is required since it is viewed freely from the public square (Place Hassan II).
