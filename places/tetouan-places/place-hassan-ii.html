Place Hassan II

The vibrant grand square connecting Tetouan's historic medina with the Spanish Ensanche.

Best Time to Go Morning & late afternoon
Time Needed 30 minutes – 2 hours
Entry Free
Where Centre of historic Tetouan

The square that bridges two eras

Place Hassan II is the grand urban heart of Tetouan, seamlessly linking the 20th-century Spanish colonial quarter (Ensanche) with the ancient gates of the UNESCO-listed medina. Framed by elegant neoclassical façades and anchored by the striking Royal Palace gates, it serves as the ultimate gathering space for locals and visitors alike.

Visit in the morning to watch the city come alive under gentle northern light, or return at sunset when families stroll across the expansive paved esplanade and local cafés fill with conversation.

Serving as your primary orientation point in Tetouan, you will cross this plaza repeatedly as you transition between museum visits, architectural tours, and deep explorations of the labyrinthine souks.

Practical Tips for Visiting Place Hassan II

Use it as your geographical compass

Keep track of the royal palace gates and medina entry points to navigate effortlessly.

Morning calm vs. evening social buzz

Early hours offer clear photographic views, whereas late afternoons showcase authentic local community life.

Café etiquette

Savoring a glass of fresh mint tea at a terrace facing the square is a quintessential experience.

Good to Know

Place Hassan II FAQ

Is Place Hassan II safe at night?

Yes, it is generally safe, busy, and well-lit in the evening. Standard city mindfulness applies.

Do I need a ticket to visit?

No, the square is entirely public and free to access.

How much time should I spend here?

Around 30 minutes for a walkthrough, or longer if you relax at a café terrace.
