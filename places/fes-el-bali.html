Fes el-Bali

The Largest Car-Free Urban Area in the World & UNESCO World Heritage Site.

About Fes el-Bali

Founded in the 8th century by the Idrissid dynasty, Fes el-Bali is the oldest walled part of Fes. Home to over 9,000 narrow streets and alleyways, it offers an extraordinary step back in time where donkeys and handcarts remain the main mode of transporting goods.

Inside this medieval maze, you will discover bustling souks, historic mosques, traditional madrasas, and lively artisan workshops specializing in leatherwork, ceramics, metalwork, and weaving.

LocationCentral Fes, Medina
Best Time to ExploreEarly Morning or Late Afternoon
