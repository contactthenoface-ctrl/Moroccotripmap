Îles Purpuraires

Rocky islands off Essaouira’s coast — best admired from the Skala, with boat access only when the Atlantic allows.

Best time Clear days from the ramparts
Suggested duration Views 30 min · boat variable
Entry Views free · boats seasonal
Location Offshore from the port

Islands on the horizon

The Îles Purpuraires (Mogador islands) sit just off Essaouira. From the Skala du Port and parts of the sea walls they form a dark, rocky silhouette against the Atlantic.

Landing is restricted and boat trips depend entirely on weather and local rules. Never pressure operators in rough seas. For most visitors, the viewpoint from the ramparts is the reliable experience.

Use the islands as a visual reward during a port and Skala morning rather than as a guaranteed excursion.

Food near the viewpoints

There are no visitor facilities on the islands — eat at the port or in the medina after your rampart viewpoint stop.

  • Port grilled fish after the Skala walk.
  • Medina cafés inland from the walls.
  • Carry water on exposed ramparts.
  • Cash for small stalls near the port.
  • Wind cools quickly after sunset on the walls.

No facilities on the islands for casual visitors.

Good to know

FAQ about the Îles Purpuraires

Can I always visit the islands?

No. Access is restricted and weather-dependent. Shore views are the dependable option.

Where is the best view?

Skala du Port and seaward sections of the medina walls.

Are boat trips safe?

Only in calm conditions with established operators — decline if the sea looks rough.

How long for views only?

Thirty minutes at the ramparts is enough for most people.

Binoculars useful?

Yes if you want more detail on the rocks and birds.
