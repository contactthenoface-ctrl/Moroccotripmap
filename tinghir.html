Welcome to Tinghir — oasis town at the foot of the Todra Gorges

Tinghir sits in a long palm oasis between the High Atlas and the Sahara road. It is the natural base for the Todra Gorges.

Explore the palmery, the old ksar, the Glaoui Kasbah and the road through the gorge toward Tamtatouchte.

Planning

Suggested 2-day plan in Tinghir

Gorges first, then oasis and villages.

Morning — Drive to the gorges

Leave Tinghir for Todra; walk the narrows.

Afternoon — Tizgui or Tamtatouchte

Viewpoints or continue up the canyon.

Evening — Back in Tinghir

Dinner in town.

Morning — Palmeraie walk

Explore gardens and irrigation channels.

Afternoon — Ksar & Glaoui Kasbah

Old quarter and hilltop view.

Evening — Souk or departure

Market streets or continue the desert route.

Good to know

Tinghir travel FAQ

How many days in Tinghir?

One full day for gorges and palmery; two nights if you prefer a slower pace.

How far are the Todra Gorges?

About 15–20 minutes by road north of Tinghir.

Good stop on the desert route?

Yes — between Ouarzazate/Dades and Erfoud/Merzouga.

Best time?

Spring and autumn are most comfortable.
