Dayet Hachlaf

A place to visit in Ifrane.

Best time Morning or late afternoon
Suggested time 1 – 2 hours
Entry Free shore access
Location Near Ifrane · Middle Atlas

A quieter alternative to the main lake circuit

Dayet Hachlaf offers a more low-key lake stop in the Ifrane area. Expect fewer facilities and a stronger sense of open Middle Atlas landscape. Ideal if you already saw Dayet Aoua and want another shoreline without repeating the same scene.

Access is by road from Ifrane; conditions depend on weather. Pack everything you need.
