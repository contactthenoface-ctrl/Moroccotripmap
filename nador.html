Nador

Where the Rif meets a vast Mediterranean lagoon: Marchica, a lively corniche, mountain viewpoints and wild headlands toward Cap des Trois Fourches.

​
Best time May – October for beaches; spring & autumn for hiking and views
Ideal stay 3 – 5 days
Vibe Lagoon, corniche, Rif coast, ferry gateway
Airport / port Nador (NDR) · Beni Ansar ferry
​

Welcome to Nador — lagoon light and Rif coast

Nador sits on Morocco’s northeastern Mediterranean shore, defined less by an old town than by water: the Marchica lagoon (Mar Chica), one of the largest saltwater lagoons in the Mediterranean basin, stretches for roughly twenty-five kilometres beside the city.

Evenings belong to the corniche. Day trips pull you toward Mount Gourougou, the dramatic Cap des Trois Fourches peninsula, quieter beaches like Charrana and Ras El Ma, and the working port of Beni Ansar. Melilla lies just across the border for a short passport day if you want the contrast.

​
Highlights

Must-visit places in Nador

Lagoon, corniche, mountain, headland and nearby beaches.

Lagoon

Marchica Lagoon (Mar Chica)

A vast Mediterranean lagoon beside the city — calm water, birdlife and the defining landscape of Nador.

​
Promenade

Nador Corniche

The city’s evening waterfront — cafés, lagoon views and the social heart of town.

​
Mountain

Mount Gourougou

A low mountain above Nador with trails and wide views over lagoon, coast and Melilla on clear days.

​
Headland

Cap des Trois Fourches

A dramatic peninsula of cliffs and empty coast — one of Morocco’s wildest Mediterranean landscapes.

​
Beach

Charrana Beach

A cliff-backed cove of clear water about an hour from town — quieter swimming away from the lagoon strip.

​
Port

Beni Ansar Port

Working harbour and ferry terminal — landings, seafood and the practical edge of the city.

​
Heritage

Kasbah of Selouane

A 17th-century Alaouite fortification inland from Nador — history with a short road hop.

​
Coast

Ras El Ma (Cap de l’Eau)

A calmer coastal town and beach east of Nador — sand, fishing boats and open Mediterranean light.

​
Planning

Suggested 3-day plan for Nador

Lagoon and corniche first, then mountain or headland, then a beach day.

Morning — Marchica viewpoints

First look at the lagoon from the corniche and open water edges.

Afternoon — Town pace

Simple lunch, optional short lagoon walk or rest before evening.

Evening — Corniche

Cafés, lagoon light and seafood when the promenade fills.

Morning — Gourougou or road west

Trail or transfer toward mountain viewpoints, or start toward Cap des Trois Fourches.

Afternoon — Views & return

Panoramas over lagoon and coast; allow time for the road back.

Evening — Quiet dinner

Town or corniche table after a long outdoor day.

Morning — Charrana or Ras El Ma

Swim and sand away from the main lagoon strip.

Afternoon — Flexible

Beni Ansar harbour energy or a second lagoon stroll.

Evening — Optional

Corniche again or early night before onward travel.

Practical tips for Nador

Getting around

Town and corniche are walkable; beaches and Cap des Trois Fourches need a car or organised transfer.

Sun & seasons

Mediterranean sun is strong in summer. Spring and autumn are ideal for Gourougou and the Cap.

Cash & border

Carry dirhams for taxis. If you day-trip to Melilla, bring a valid passport and check border hours.

Around Nador

Best outings from Nador

Cap des Trois Fourches

Half or full day to cliffs, empty coast and viewpoints.

Mount Gourougou

Short hikes and panoramas over Nador and lagoon.

Charrana or Ras El Ma

Beach day with clearer swimming than the inner lagoon.

Melilla (passport day)

Spanish enclave across the border with modernist streets.

​
Good to know

Nador travel FAQ

How many days in Nador?

Three days cover the lagoon, corniche and one mountain or beach outing.

Is Nador only the lagoon?

Marchica is the signature landscape, but Gourougou and Cap des Trois Fourches add wild coast.

How do I get to Nador?

Flights to NDR, ferries via Beni Ansar, or road links along the Rif.

Best time to visit?

May to October for swimming and corniche evenings.
