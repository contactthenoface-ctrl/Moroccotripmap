Agadir Beach

Wide Atlantic sand backed by resort hotels, palm trees and a lively corniche.

Best Time to Go Morning or sunset
Time Needed 2 – 4 hours
Entry Free
Area Bay & Corniche

The Long Curve of the Atlantic Bay

Agadir Beach is the pulse of the city — a broad, magnificent crescent of golden sand stretching for kilometres along the Atlantic Ocean. Backed by a palm-lined pedestrian corniche and major resort hotels, it offers a relaxing beach resort atmosphere with plenty of space for everyone.

Whether you want to take a refreshing morning walk, rent a sun lounger for a lazy afternoon, or watch the sun dip below the horizon with the hilltop Kasbah glowing in the distance, the beach is always inviting.

Water sports such as jet-skiing, paddleboarding, and camel rides along the shoreline are readily available, making it vibrant yet easy to enjoy at your own pace.

Practical Tips for Visiting Agadir Beach

Sun Protection

Agadir boasts over 300 days of sunshine. Bring high SPF sunscreen, sunglasses and stay hydrated.

Sections & Crowds

Hotel fronts are busier with loungers; walking further north or south yields quieter sandy stretches.

Safety

The beach is generally very safe and patrolled. Keep normal care of personal belongings when swimming.

Good to Know

Agadir Beach FAQ

Is Agadir Beach safe for swimming?

Yes. Main stretches in front of hotels and beach clubs are supervised by lifeguards. Always respect flag warnings regarding Atlantic currents.

Can I rent loungers and umbrellas?

Yes, sun loungers and parasols are widely available for daily rent along the public beach and in front of beach clubs.

What is the best time to visit the beach?

Early morning is peaceful for walking or jogging. Late afternoon is ideal for catching the sunset over the Atlantic bay.
