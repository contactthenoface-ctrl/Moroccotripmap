Mastering Transportation Across the Kingdom

Morocco boasts one of Africa's most modern and efficient transportation networks. From Al Boraq—Africa's first ultra-high-speed train connecting Tangier, Rabat, and Casablanca in under two hours—to smooth modern highways winding through the High Atlas, traveling across the country is comfortable and accessible.

Whether you prefer the freedom of self-driving a 4x4 into Sahara dunes, reserving a hassle-free door-to-door private airport shuttle, or riding iconic petit taxis through vibrant medinas, our guide helps you choose the best route for your itinerary and budget.

Practical Travel Advice

Frequently Asked Questions About Transport in Morocco

Essential tips on train tickets, car hire requirements, driver licenses, and airport transfer tips.

Do I need an International Driving Permit (IDP) to rent a car in Morocco?

In most cases, a valid driver's license from your home country (written in Roman letters, such as EU, US, UK, Canadian) is accepted for tourist car rentals up to 90 days. An International Driving Permit is recommended for extra clarity during police checkpoints.

How far in advance should I book Al Boraq high-speed train tickets?

We recommend booking ONCF Al Boraq tickets at least 3 to 7 days in advance online to secure cheap promo fares and first-class seats, especially during weekend peak hours between Tangier, Rabat, and Casablanca.

What is the difference between Petit Taxis and Grand Taxis?

Petit Taxis operate exclusively within city borders, carry up to 3 passengers, and use a fare meter (compteur). Grand Taxis operate regional routes between towns or to airports, carry up to 6 passengers, and charge fixed per-seat fares.

Why pre-book private airport transfers instead of taking an airport taxi?

Pre-booked airport transfers guarantee fixed upfront prices, flight tracking for delays, luggage assistance, and direct door-to-door escort to your Riad inside car-free medina alleys without price haggling after a long flight.
