Sidi Ifni

Art Deco Atlantic town — Spanish legacy, fishing port and Legzira arches on Morocco's southern coast.

Best time October – April · mild Atlantic climate
Ideal stay 1 – 2 nights
Atmosphere Art Deco · Coast · Quiet town
Access Agadir (AGA) ≈ 2.5–3h · via Tiznit · grands taxis

Welcome to Sidi Ifni — Art Deco on the Atlantic

Sidi Ifni sits on Morocco's southern Atlantic coast, about two and a half hours south of Agadir via Tiznit. Until 1969 it was a Spanish enclave (Ifni). That past still shows in the white and blue Art Deco buildings, the former Plaza de España and a laid-back fishing-town rhythm rarely found on busier coastal strips.

The town itself is compact: walk the colonial centre, watch the port, then day-trip to Legzira's natural stone arches a short drive north. Ideal for one or two nights between Tiznit, Mirleft and the road toward Guelmim or the Anti-Atlas.

Heritage

Art Deco centre & Plaza

Sidi Ifni's centre still carries the geometry of the Spanish period: curved balconies, white walls, blue details and a main square long known as Plaza de España. Walk slowly — the charm is in the façades and the quiet scale, not in museums.

Morning light is soft on the white buildings; late afternoon warms the stone. Cafés around the square are natural pauses between short loops of the old streets.

Combine the centre walk with the port in the same half-day.

Port

Fishing port

The fishing port is the working edge of Sidi Ifni. Boats, nets and the day's catch set a different pace from the Art Deco streets above. It is a place to watch rather than a polished tourist stop.

Late afternoon is lively when boats return. Respect the work areas and ask before photographing people closely.

Good light near sunset; keep a respectful distance from active docks.

Nature

Legzira beach & arches

Legzira, a short drive north of Sidi Ifni, is famous for its natural stone arches on the beach. Tide and light change the scene hour by hour — the classic postcard view is best checked against local tide timing.

Reach it by grand taxi, shared transport or private car. Wear shoes you can walk on sand and wet rock; facilities are limited so bring water.

Go for low-to-mid tide if you want to walk closer to the arches. Sunset is popular — arrive early.

Coast

Sidi Ifni beach & corniche

Sidi Ifni's own beach stretches along the Atlantic with a simple corniche. It is less dramatic than Legzira but ideal for an evening walk without leaving town.

Swimming depends on swell and local advice — the Atlantic here can be strong. Sunsets over the water are the main daily show.

Sunset walk on the corniche after dinner in town.

Heritage

Spanish church & civic buildings

Several buildings from the Spanish period remain: the former church and civic structures that once served the enclave. They are part of the open-air story of Ifni rather than a single ticketed site.

Access and condition can vary; treat them as heritage landmarks on a walking loop of the centre.

Pair with the Plaza and Art Deco streets in one easy circuit.

Nearby

Mirleft (day trip)

Mirleft lies on the coast between Tiznit and Sidi Ifni. It has become a small surf and café stop with a more village feel than a large resort.

Easy as a half-day or a night if you want beach time between the silver city and Art Deco Sidi Ifni.

Combine with the road trip from Tiznit if you are driving south.

Practical tips for Sidi Ifni

Getting around

The centre is walkable. Grands taxis link to Tiznit, Legzira and Guelmim; a car helps for beach hops.

Legzira tides

Check tide timing if you want to walk near the arches. Wear sturdy shoes.

When to come

October–April is milder. Summers are hot and dry with a strong sun.

Good to know

Questions about Sidi Ifni

How long should I stay in Sidi Ifni?

One night is enough for the centre and a Legzira visit; two nights if you want a slower pace and Mirleft or extra beach time.

Is Legzira worth it from Sidi Ifni?

Yes — it is the main nature highlight of the area. Plan around tide and light if you care about photos of the arches.

How do I get to Sidi Ifni?

Most visitors come by road from Agadir via Tiznit (about 2.5–3 hours). Grands taxis and buses serve the route; a rental car is flexible for Legzira and Mirleft.

Is Sidi Ifni good for swimming?

The Atlantic can be rough. Prefer calm days, follow local advice, and treat Legzira mainly as a walk and photo stop rather than a swim beach.
