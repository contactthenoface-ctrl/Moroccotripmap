Tangier Beach

City sand and corniche near the new town.

Best time
Morning or late afternoon
Suggested duration
1 – 2 hours
Entry
Free
Location
City seafront / corniche

Sea level after the medina climbs

Tangier’s main city beach stretches along the bay with a corniche for walking. It is not a remote cove — expect urban energy, hotels nearby and a practical place to breathe after medina lanes.

Swimming depends on conditions and local advice; many visitors come for the walk and the horizon toward Spain on clear days.

Practical tips

Getting there

Petit taxi or walk from the new town; hotels often sit on or near the strip.

Swimming

Check local conditions; this is an urban beach, not a guarded resort bay.

Combine

Good reset between medina mornings and evening plans.

Good to know

FAQ about Tangier Beach

Is the beach clean enough to swim?

Conditions vary; many prefer walking and views. Ask locally on the day.

How long is the seafront?

A long urban stretch — enough for a solid walk without leaving the city.

Far from the medina?

A short taxi or a longer walk depending on your medina exit.

Best time of day?

Morning for cooler air; evening for the promenade atmosphere.

Family friendly?

Yes for walks and space; supervise near the water.
