Tangier Medina

Whitewashed lanes, café squares and the historic heart between the port and the kasbah hill.

Best time Morning or late afternoon
Suggested duration 2 – 3 hours
Entry Free to wander
Location Old town / medina
Overview

A port medina with Atlantic and Mediterranean light

Tangier medina climbs from the port edge toward the kasbah. Unlike inland medinas, the sea is never far — you catch it between walls, from rooftops and at the lower gates. Petit Socco remains the classic café pause; Grand Socco is the open square that links old town and new.

Wander without a rigid route: alleys shift from quiet residential stretches to small shops and viewpoints. Pair the medina with the kasbah museum above and Café Hafa on the nearby cliffs for a full half-day.

Good to know

FAQ about Tangier Medina

Is the medina safe to walk?

Yes for daytime wandering on main routes; keep valuables discreet and use a map offline.

Grand Socco vs Petit Socco?

Grand Socco is the large outer square; Petit Socco is the smaller café square deeper in the medina.

How long do I need?

Two to three hours covers lanes, a café stop and the climb toward the kasbah.

Best entry point?

From Grand Socco or the port-side gates — both feed into the main lanes.

Combine with what?

Kasbah Museum, Café Hafa and the American Legation sit on or near the same loop.
