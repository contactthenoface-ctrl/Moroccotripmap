Bahia Palace

A 19th-century masterpiece of marble, zellige tilework, painted cedarwood, and quiet courtyards.

Best Time to Go8:30 AM Opening
Time Needed1 – 1.5 Hours
Entry Fee70 MAD
LocationMellah / Southern Medina

The Brilliance of Moroccan Royal Crafts

Built in two phases by Si Moussa, grand vizier of Sultan Muhammad ibn Abd al-Rahman, and later expanded by his son Ba Ahmed, the Bahia Palace ("Palace of Brilliance") was intended to be the greatest palace of its time.

Spanning nearly 8 hectares, the complex connects intricate courtyards, riad gardens, secret reception halls, and private apartments. Top artisans from Fez and across Morocco were brought in to carve stuccos, paint cedar roofs, and lay geometric zellige tiles.
