Foum El Oued Beach

Where the Sahara meets the Atlantic: a long Blue Flag sandy coastline, ocean breezes, and golden sunsets just 25 km from Laâyoune.

Best Time to Go Morning & Late Afternoon
Time Needed Half Day – Full Day
Entry Free, Open Access
Where 25 km southwest of Laâyoune

The Atlantic Escape of the Moroccan Sahara

Foum El Oued Beach is the main seaside outlet for Laâyoune residents and visitors. Stretching over seven kilometres of fine Atlantic sand, this Blue Flag beach (awarded for many consecutive years) combines clean waters, a landscaped promenade, and the unique landscape of desert dunes meeting the ocean.

Located about 25 kilometres southwest of the city centre, it is easily reached by grand taxi or private car in around 20–25 minutes. The beach offers lifeguard zones in summer, parking, sanitary facilities, and a relaxed family atmosphere.

Whether you come for a quiet morning walk, kitesurfing when the wind picks up, or a sunset tea by the water, Foum El Oued delivers one of the most authentic coastal experiences in southern Morocco.

Practical Tips for Visiting Foum El Oued Beach

Getting There

Grand taxis leave regularly from Laâyoune city centre. The ride takes 20–25 minutes and costs a modest fixed fare. Private taxis and rental cars are also easy options.

Swimming & Safety

The Atlantic can have strong currents. Swim only in supervised zones when lifeguards are present. Always check local flags and conditions. Children should be closely supervised.

What to Bring

Strong sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, water, and a light jacket for the evening breeze. Facilities include parking, toilets, and basic showers in season.

Good to Know

Foum El Oued Beach FAQ

Is Foum El Oued Beach free to visit?

Yes, the beach is completely free and open to the public year-round. Parking is also free or very inexpensive.

How far is the beach from Laâyoune city centre?

Approximately 25 kilometres (about 20–25 minutes by taxi or car).

Is it safe to swim at Foum El Oued?

Swimming is possible in designated areas, especially in summer when lifeguards are present. The Atlantic can have strong currents, so always follow local advice and flags.

What is the best time of year to visit?

October to April for pleasant temperatures. Summer is popular with locals but can be hotter and busier.

Are there restaurants and facilities on the beach?

Basic facilities (toilets, showers in season, parking) and simple seafood stands are available. For more options, visit El Marsa Port or return to the city.
