Frequently Asked Questions about Stays in Morocco

Everything you need to know about booking riads, hotels, and desert camps.

What is the difference between a traditional Riad and a Dar?

A Riad traditionally features an interior garden or courtyard with large citrus or palm trees open to the sky. A Dar is typically a smaller traditional house built around a central tiled light-well or patio without large garden trees. Both offer exceptional privacy and authentic Moroccan architecture.

Is breakfast included when booking Moroccan riads?

Yes! Almost all traditional riads and guesthouses across Morocco include a generous, homemade breakfast featuring fresh orange juice, Moroccan mint tea, msemen flatbreads, honey, homemade jams, and eggs served on the rooftop terrace or courtyard.

Should I book my desert camp in Merzouga in advance?

Absolutely. Luxury desert camps in the Erg Chebbi dunes are extremely popular, especially during spring and autumn peak seasons. Booking through reliable platforms like Booking.com ensures you secure your preferred tent category and camel trek options ahead of time.

Are riads accessible by car within the medinas?

Most medinas (such as Fes el-Bali or Marrakech Medina) are pedestrian-only zones. Riads are located down historic alleys. However, most properties offer luggage assistance from the nearest guarded parking lot or medina gate upon arrival.
