FAQ — El Hart Pottery

How much time for El Hart Pottery?

Plan at least 1–3 hours.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes with supervision near cliffs and uneven paths.

Do I need a guide?

Not required to visit workshops; a local contact helps if you want demonstrations or bulk purchases.

How to get there?

By road from Tinghir on the desert route.

Best season?

Year-round; morning quieter
