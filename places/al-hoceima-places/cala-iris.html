Cala Iris

Fishing village and wild cove on the Al Hoceima National Park shoreline.

Best time Full half-day outing
Suggested duration Half day
Entry Free shore; respect park rules
Location Park coast west of town

Village and wild water

Cala Iris is a small fishing settlement and cove along the national park coast — a clear step away from Al Hoceima’s urban beaches into a quieter Rif Mediterranean shoreline.

The draw is the setting: boats, clear water and cliffs rather than city services. It works well as the anchor of a park-coast day with a driver or organised transfer.

Bring what you need for sun and swimming. Facilities are limited compared with Quemado or Sfiha.

Food near Cala Iris

Plan meals in Al Hoceima town or at seasonal beach cafés; pack water for park-coast outings.

  • Grilled fish near the port and corniche.
  • Simple cafés at Quemado and Sfiha in season.
  • Carry water and snacks for Cala Iris, Torres and Bades.
  • Hotel restaurants if you stay on the bay.
  • Confirm opening hours outside peak summer.

Cash helps at small beach and village stops.

Good to know

Cala Iris FAQ

How far from Al Hoceima?

A coastal drive west into the park zone — confirm duration with your driver.

Worth it without a car?

Yes if you arrange a taxi or tour; public options may be limited.

Swimming?

Possible in calm conditions; this is a more natural cove than town beaches.

Combine with Torres de Alcalá?

Yes — both sit on the broader park coast and pair well in one day.

Food on site?

Very limited — pack water and snacks or eat before/after in town.
