Casablanca

Morocco’s economic capital where the world’s tallest minaret meets Art Deco boulevards and Atlantic sunsets.

Best Time to Visit Apr – Jun & Sep – Nov
Ideal Stay 2 – 4 Days
Top Vibe Modern & Coastal
Nearest Airport CMN (Mohammed V)

Welcome to Morocco’s White City

Casablanca is not the Morocco of postcard riads and snake charmers. It is a working, breathing metropolis of more than three million people, where Art Deco facades from the French Protectorate era stand beside glass towers, and the Atlantic crashes against the walls of the Hassan II Mosque. For many travellers it is simply the gateway, but those who stay discover a city of genuine character: excellent seafood, a surprisingly compact historic core, and a coastline that locals treat as their living room.

Whether you come for the breathtaking interior of Africa’s largest mosque, a sunset walk along the Corniche, or a day trip to the quiet capital of Rabat, Casablanca rewards curiosity. It is Morocco at its most contemporary — and still deeply Moroccan.

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A balanced schedule to discover the highlights without rushing your visit.

Day 1

Mosque & Old City

Morning guided tour of the Hassan II Mosque, then walk through the Old Medina and finish with sunset on the Corniche.

Full Day • Centre & Coast
Day 2

Art Deco & Habous

Explore Place Mohammed V and the downtown architecture, then spend the afternoon in the atmospheric Quartier Habous.

Full Day • Downtown & Habous

Day Trip or Coast

Take the train to Rabat for the Kasbah and Hassan Tower, or stay local and enjoy seafood and beach clubs along Ain Diab.

Practical Tips for Visiting Casablanca

Getting Around

The Casa Tramway is clean, cheap and useful for many central routes. For longer distances use red petit taxis (insist on the meter) or rideshare apps. The airport train is the easiest way from CMN.

Dress & Etiquette

Casablanca is more relaxed than inland cities, but modest clothing is still appreciated, especially when visiting the mosque. Bring a light jacket — the Atlantic breeze can be cool even in summer.

Safety & Atmosphere

Casablanca is a big city. Use normal big-city awareness, especially after dark in quieter streets. The Corniche and main tourist areas feel lively and safe in the evening.

Day Trips

Best Day Trips from Casablanca

Easy train or car excursions that fit comfortably into a single day.

Rabat

Morocco’s elegant capital — Kasbah of the Udayas, Hassan Tower and Chellah, only 1 hour by train.

El Jadida

UNESCO Portuguese citadel and the famous underground cistern, about 1h20 by train or car.

Mohammedia

Closest beach escape — clean sand, marina and seafood restaurants, only 25–30 minutes away.

Azemmour

Quiet historic town with colourful street art and river views, often combined with El Jadida.

Good to Know

Casablanca Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Casablanca?

Two full days cover the main sights comfortably. Three or four days allow a relaxed pace plus a day trip to Rabat or El Jadida.

Is Casablanca safe for tourists?

Yes. Like any large city, use normal precautions. Tourist areas, the Corniche and the centre are generally well-frequented and feel safe.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; keep some cash for taxis, markets and smaller shops.

How do I get from Mohammed V Airport to the city centre?

The ONCF airport train is the most convenient option (about 35–45 minutes). Official taxis and private transfers are also available; agree the price or use the meter.
