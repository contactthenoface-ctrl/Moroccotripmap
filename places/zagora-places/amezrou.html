Amezrou

Earthen lanes and the old Jewish quarter — a walkable heritage corner of the Zagora oasis.

Best time Morning or late afternoon
Suggested duration 1 – 2 hours
Entry Free to walk; tips for guides
Location Edge of Zagora oasis

Lanes of earth and memory

Amezrou is the historic oasis village side of Zagora: narrow lanes, earthen walls and traces of the old Jewish quarter that once formed an integral part of the town’s social and craft fabric.

It is best discovered on foot. A short walk gives insight into traditional Draa Valley architecture and daily life away from the main road strip.

Stay respectful in residential alleys and ask for permission before photographing locals or private doorways.

Practical tips for Amezrou

Access

Easily reached on foot or by a short bicycle ride from central Zagora.

Sun Protection

Bring water, a hat, and sunscreen, especially when walking in warmer months.

Respect

Respect local residents' privacy in narrow alleys and residential quarters.

Good to know

Amezrou FAQ

How far from the centre?

It's located right on the edge of town, just a short walk or taxi ride from central Zagora.

Is a guide needed?

Not strictly required, but a local guide adds great value regarding the history of the old Jewish quarter and artisan traditions.
