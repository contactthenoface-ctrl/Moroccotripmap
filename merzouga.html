Merzouga

Gateway to the Sahara and Erg Chebbi dunes: camel treks, desert camps and Saharan horizons — Merzouga is the door between the Atlas and the dunes.

Best Time to Visit Apr – Jun & Sep – Nov
Ideal Stay 2 – 3 Days
Top Vibe Desert & Dunes
Nearest Airport ERH (Errachidia)

Welcome to the Sahara Gateway

Merzouga sits on the edge of the majestic Erg Chebbi dunes. Kasbahs, desert tracks and starry horizons define the area. It is the ultimate base for Erg Chebbi, camel treks and overnight desert camps.

Two to three days cover the dunes, a camel trek and a night in camp — plus Khamlia, Dayet Srji or Rissani oasis if you stay longer. Use Merzouga as a highlight on a Moroccan desert circuit.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

Dunes, a night in camp, and the villages around Erg Chebbi.

Morning — Erg Chebbi viewpoint

Drive or walk to the edge of Erg Chebbi. Climb a dune for the first panoramic view before the heat peaks.

Afternoon — Merzouga village & fossils

Return to the village for lunch, fossil shops, and time to arrange your camel trek and camp.

Evening — Sunset on the dunes

Watch sunset from a dune ridge, then dinner at your hotel or a village restaurant.

Morning — Free or short walk

A quiet morning in the village or Hassilabied; rest before the afternoon trek.

Afternoon — Camel trek into Erg Chebbi

Depart as arranged with your camp: camel trek into the dunes toward your overnight stay.

Evening — Night in the Sahara

Berber dinner, drums and stars; sleep in a tent or camp with optional sunrise climb.

Morning — Sunrise & return

Climb for sunrise, breakfast at camp, then camel or 4x4 back to the village.

Afternoon — Khamlia or Rissani

Gnawa music and tea in Khamlia, or Rissani’s souk and Moulay Ali Cherif mausoleum.

Evening — Last night in Merzouga

Quiet dinner before onward travel toward Erfoud, Tinghir or Ouarzazate.

Practical Tips for Visiting Merzouga

Getting Around

The nearest airport is Errachidia (ERH), about a 2-hour drive away. 4x4 vehicles and private transfers are ideal for exploring the desert tracks around Merzouga.

Dress & Etiquette

Wear light, breathable clothing during the day and pack warm layers for chilly desert nights. A scarf or head wrap is essential for protection against sand and sun.

Safety & Atmosphere

Merzouga is very welcoming and safe for travellers. Always stay hydrated, drink bottled water, and book desert camps through trusted operators.

Good to Know

Merzouga Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Merzouga?

Two full days cover the main sights comfortably. Three or four days allow a relaxed pace plus a day trip to Rissani or surrounding oases.

Is Merzouga safe for tourists?

Yes. Merzouga is very safe and welcoming. Always book your desert camps and guides through verified local operators.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Cards are accepted in larger hotels, but keep cash handy for small shops, tips and local markets.

How do I get to Merzouga from the nearest airport?

The closest airport is Errachidia (ERH), about a 2-hour drive. Private transfers, taxis or rental cars are readily available to take you directly to the dunes.
