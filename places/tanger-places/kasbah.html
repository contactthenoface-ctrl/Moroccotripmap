Kasbah Museum

Palace-museum and viewpoints on the hill above the medina, facing the Strait of Gibraltar.

Best time Morning or late afternoon
Suggested duration 1 – 1.5 hours
Entry Paid museum ticket
Location Kasbah hill, medina heights
Overview

The hill that watches the strait

The Kasbah Museum occupies a former sultan’s palace at the top of Tangier medina. Rooms display crafts and history; terraces and gardens open toward the Strait of Gibraltar and the roofs below.

Walk up through the medina or take a petit taxi to the kasbah gate. Combine with Café Hafa on the Marshan cliffs for tea with a wider sea view after the museum.

Good to know

FAQ about Kasbah Museum

Is the kasbah free?

Outdoor approaches are walkable; the museum itself has a paid ticket.

How long is the visit?

About one to one and a half hours including terraces.

Are the views worth it?

Yes — one of the best elevated looks at the strait and medina together.

Hard climb?

Moderate slopes and steps; a taxi can drop you near the gate.

Best time of day?

Late afternoon for outdoor light; morning for quieter galleries.
