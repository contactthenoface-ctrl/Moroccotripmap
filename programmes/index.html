Morocco Travel Itineraries: Select Your Region & Duration

Explore multi-city circuits connecting Casablanca, Rabat, Fes, Marrakech, Tangier, Chefchaouen, Essaouira, Agadir, and the Sahara. Select a region and filter by 5, 7, or 10 days for expert routes with realistic driving times.

5 Days / 4 Nights

5-Day Express Triangle: Casablanca, Rabat & Marrakech

Ideal for short stays or business travelers adding a cultural getaway. Connect Morocco's economic capital with its coastal administrative seat and the vibrant Red City via high-speed train and smooth highway.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Key Stops Casablanca ➔ Rabat ➔ Marrakech
Ideal Transport Al Boraq High-Speed Train + Private City Driver
Traveler Profile Short-stay visitors, Business Travelers, Couples
Estimated Budget Mid-range to High-end

Stage Timeline

Day 1

Casablanca Oceanfront & Modern Heritage

Visit the majestic Hassan II Mosque built over the Atlantic, wander through Habous Quarter, and enjoy dinner along the Corniche.

Discover Casablanca city guides and monuments →
Day 2

Rabat Imperial Capital & Kasbah of the Udayas

Take the 45-minute Al Boraq train to Rabat. Visit Hassan Tower, the Royal Mausoleum, and stroll through the cliffside blue Kasbah of the Udayas.

Book high-speed Al Boraq train tickets →
Days 3 to 5

Marrakech Medina Immersion & Departure

Head south to Marrakech. Spend two full days exploring Bahia Palace, Majorelle Garden, historical souks, and dining overlooking Jemaa el-Fna.

Explore complete Marrakech riad and activity options →

Local Expert Tip: Take the morning Al Boraq train between Casablanca and Rabat to avoid highway rush-hour traffic and enjoy seamless city-center-to-city-center transfers.

7 Days / 6 Nights

7-Day Imperial Capitals Circuit: Casablanca, Rabat, Fes & Marrakech

The classic grand tour connecting all major historical powerhouses of Morocco. Trace centuries of royal dynasties from Atlantic ports to medieval medinas.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Key Stops Casablanca ➔ Rabat ➔ Meknes & Volubilis ➔ Fes ➔ Marrakech
Ideal Transport Rental Car or Private Chauffeur
Traveler Profile Culture Lovers, History Buffs, Families
Estimated Budget Mid-range to Luxury

Stage Timeline

Days 1 & 2

Casablanca Modernity & Rabat Royal Heritage

Marvel at Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca before heading to Rabat to tour Chellah Necropolis and the Kasbah of the Udayas.

Days 3 & 4

Roman Volubilis, Meknes & Fes Medina

Visit the UNESCO Roman ruins of Volubilis and imperial Meknes gate Bab Mansour before diving into the maze-like medina of Fes.

Days 5 to 7

High Atlas Scenic Drive to Marrakech

Travel south to Marrakech for two days of artisan shopping, historical palaces, and culinary experiences.

Local Expert Tip: Hire an official local guide in Fes to easily navigate the 9,000 alleys of Fes el-Bali without getting lost.

10 Days / 9 Nights

10-Day Complete Grand Tour: Imperial Cities & Merzouga Sahara

The comprehensive Moroccan expedition combining Casablanca, Rabat, Fes, the Middle Atlas cedar forests, Merzouga desert dunes, Dades Gorges, and Marrakech.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 10 Days / 9 Nights
Key Stops Casablanca ➔ Rabat ➔ Fes ➔ Ifrane ➔ Merzouga Desert ➔ Dades ➔ Marrakech
Ideal Transport 4x4 SUV or Private Minivan with Chauffeur
Traveler Profile First-time Explorers, Couples, Photographers
Estimated Budget Mid-range to Luxury

Stage Timeline

Days 1 to 3

Casablanca, Rabat & Fes Medieval Heritage

Start in Casablanca, visit Rabat's royal landmarks, and drive to Fes for an immersive day exploring artisans and ancient madrassas.

Days 4 & 5

Middle Atlas, Ziz Valley & Sahara Bivouac

Drive through Ifrane and the Ziz Valley palm groves to Erg Chebbi. Ride camels into the dunes at sunset and spend a luxury night in a desert camp.

Days 6 to 10

Gorges, Kasbah Aït Benhaddou & Marrakech

Travel along the Road of 1,000 Kasbahs, stop at Aït Benhaddou, cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass, and end with 3 vibrant days in Marrakech.

Local Expert Tip: Pack a warm jacket for Merzouga nights, even in spring and autumn, as desert temperatures drop significantly after sunset.

5 Days / 4 Nights

5-Day Northern Escape: Tangier, Chefchaouen & Asilah

Where Mediterranean breeze meets Andalusian heritage. Experience literary Tangier, the artists' coastal retreat of Asilah, and the blue-washed alleyways of Chefchaouen.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Key Stops Tangier ➔ Cap Spartel ➔ Asilah ➔ Chefchaouen
Ideal Transport Rental Car or Private Transfer
Traveler Profile Photographers, Couples, Art & Nature Enthusiasts
Estimated Budget Budget to Mid-range

Stage Timeline

Days 1 & 2

Tangier Kasbah & Asilah Ocean Walls

Explore Tangier's Kasbah and Hercules Caves, then drive to coastal Asilah for white-washed street art and seafood.

Days 3 to 5

Rif Mountain Scenic Drive & Blue Chefchaouen

Head up into the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen. Spend two peaceful days photographing blue medina streets and hiking to the Spanish Mosque for sunset.

Local Expert Tip: Visit Chefchaouen early in the morning before day-trip tour buses arrive to capture undisturbed photos of the blue alleys.

7 Days / 6 Nights

7-Day Northern Highlights & Fes Medina Combination

Combine the bohemian northern coast of Tangier and Chefchaouen with the spiritual heartland of Fes and Roman Volubilis.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Key Stops Tangier ➔ Chefchaouen ➔ Akchour Waterfalls ➔ Volubilis ➔ Fes
Ideal Transport Rental Car or Private Driver
Traveler Profile Hikers, Culture Enthusiasts, Photographers
Estimated Budget Mid-range

Stage Timeline

Days 1 & 2

Tangier Port & Coastal Exploration

Discover Tangier's Grand Socco, Petit Socco, and enjoy oceanfront dining facing the Strait of Gibraltar.

Days 3 & 4

Chefchaouen & Akchour Waterfalls Trek

Explore Chefchaouen and take a day hike through Talassemtane National Park to the natural arch and waterfalls of Akchour.

Days 5 to 7

Volubilis Roman Mosaics & Fes Medina

Drive south past Moulay Idriss Zerhoun to Volubilis ruins, concluding with two immersive days in Fes.

Local Expert Tip: Wear sturdy hiking shoes for Akchour waterfalls, as the trail involves river crossings on rocks.

7 Days / 6 Nights

7-Day Sahara Desert Express: Marrakech to Merzouga & Fes

The ultimate southern route crossing the High Atlas mountains to Kasbah Aït Benhaddou, Dades Gorges, and camel trekking in Erg Chebbi before ending in Fes.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Key Stops Marrakech ➔ Tichka Pass ➔ Ouarzazate ➔ Dades ➔ Merzouga Desert ➔ Fes
Ideal Transport 4x4 SUV with Chauffeur or Rental Car
Traveler Profile First-time Visitors, Couples, Families
Estimated Budget Mid-range to Luxury

Stage Timeline

Jours 1 & 2

Marrakech & Tizi n'Tichka Atlas Pass

Explore Marrakech medina, then cross the 2,260m Tizi n'Tichka pass to tour UNESCO Kasbah Aït Benhaddou and Ouarzazate film studios.

Days 3 & 4

Dades Gorges & Merzouga Sunset Bivouac

Drive through Rose Valley and Todra Gorges. Reach Merzouga for a sunset camel trek into Erg Chebbi and spend the night in a desert camp.

Days 5 to 7

Ziz Valley Palm Groves & Fes Medina

Head north along Ziz Valley and cedar forests of Ifrane to finish with two days exploring the historic city of Fes.

Local Expert Tip: Buy dates directly from palm grove stalls along the Ziz Valley for the freshest local variety (Medjool).

10 Days / 9 Nights

10-Day Deep Southern Expedition: High Atlas Trek, Draâ Valley & Erg Chigaga

An off-the-beaten-path road trip for true adventurers. Combine trekking in Imlil near Mount Toubkal, exploring Skoura oasis, and off-road 4x4 tracks to wild Erg Chigaga dunes.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 10 Days / 9 Nights
Key Stops Marrakech ➔ Imlil (Toubkal) ➔ Skoura Oasis ➔ Draâ Valley ➔ Erg Chigaga ➔ Taroudant
Ideal Transport All-Terrain 4x4 Vehicle Mandatory
Traveler Profile Adventurers, Hikers, Photographers, Eco-Travelers
Estimated Budget Budget to Mid-range

Stage Timeline

Days 1 to 3

High Atlas Toubkal Trek & Berber Homestay

Trek through Imlil valley with a certified local guide and stay in traditional mountain villages clinging to cliff faces.

Days 4 & 5

Skoura Palm Grove & Draâ Valley Oasis

Visit Kasbah Amridil in Skoura and follow Morocco's longest palm ribbon down to Zagora and M'Hamid El Ghizlane.

Days 6 to 10

Off-Road Erg Chigaga Track & Taroudant

Take a 3-hour off-road 4x4 drive to wild Erg Chigaga dunes. Return via Taliouine saffron village and walled Taroudant to Marrakech or Agadir.

Local Expert Tip: A 4x4 driver experienced in dune driving is crucial for Erg Chigaga, as there are no paved roads across the hamada desert.

5 Days / 4 Nights

5-Day Atlantic Breeze: Essaouira, Imsouane & Taghazout Surf Spots

A relaxed seaside road trip blending artistic Mogador (Essaouira) with coastal argan forests, longboard surfing at Imsouane Magic Bay, and rooftop yoga in Taghazout.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Key Stops Essaouira ➔ N1 Coastal Road ➔ Imsouane ➔ Taghazout ➔ Agadir
Ideal Transport Compact Rental Car or Private Transfers
Traveler Profile Surfers, Solo Travelers, Couples, Wellness Seekers
Estimated Budget Flexible (Hostels to Luxury Beach Resorts)

Stage Timeline

Days 1 & 2

Essaouira Medina & Fresh Seafood Port

Walk the Portuguese ramparts, taste fresh fish grilled at port stalls, and listen to Gnaoua street music in the medina.

Day 3

Argan Cooperative Road & Imsouane Magic Bay

Drive south past women-run argan cooperatives. Pause in Imsouane to surf Africa's longest wave.

Days 4 & 5

Taghazout Yoga, Surf & Sunset Cafes

Unwind in Taghazout village with sunrise yoga, beach surf lessons, and oceanfront dining before heading to Agadir Airport.

Local Expert Tip: Purchase authentic argan oil directly from certified women's cooperatives along the N1 road between Essaouira and Agadir to support local female artisans.

7 Days / 6 Nights

7-Day Marrakech & Atlantic Ocean Combo: Souks to Surf

Combine the vibrant energy of Marrakech's palaces and souks with a refreshing coastal drive to Essaouira and Taghazout.

Criterion Detail & Recommendation
Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Key Stops Marrakech ➔ Essaouira ➔ Taghazout ➔ Paradise Valley ➔ Agadir
Ideal Transport Rental Car or Supratours Bus + Private Driver
Traveler Profile Couples, Friends, First-time Visitors seeking variety
Estimated Budget Mid-range

Stage Timeline

Days 1 to 3

Marrakech Medina, Palaces & Hammam

Immerse yourself in Marrakech, tour the Secret Garden, shop for spices, and enjoy a traditional Moroccan hammam bath.

Days 4 & 5

Coastal Essaouira & Argan Forests

Drive 2.5 hours west to Essaouira. Walk along Skala de la Ville, explore art galleries, and enjoy fresh oysters by the sea.

Days 6 & 7

Taghazout Beach & Paradise Valley Hike

Head south to Taghazout. Take a day trip to the natural rock pools of Paradise Valley before flying back from Agadir.

Local Expert Tip: The comfortable Supratours bus offers direct daily transfers between Marrakech and Essaouira for under $12 USD.
