Living Craft

Chouara Tannery

The Chouara Tannery is one of the most recognizable craft sites in Fes. From nearby terraces, visitors can look down over the traditional dyeing and leatherworking vats that have become a symbol of the city’s artisan heritage.

Leather production in Fes involves several stages, from preparing hides to tanning, dyeing and finishing. The work is still carried out by hand in workshops around the medina, although modern practices and conditions continue to evolve.

The best views are generally from surrounding leather shops and terraces. You may be approached by shopkeepers offering access, so be clear about whether you want to browse or simply see the tannery.

The smell can be strong, especially in warm weather. A small sprig of mint is sometimes offered locally, but comfortable pacing and fresh air are the simplest solutions.
