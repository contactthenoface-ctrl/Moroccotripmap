Cap Malabata

Eastern headland above Tangier bay.

Best time
Clear days for long views
Suggested duration
45 – 90 minutes
Entry
Free outdoor viewpoints
Location
East of Tangier bay

Looking back at the city and the strait

Cap Malabata is the eastern counterpart to Cap Spartel’s western drama. From the headland you look back toward Tangier’s bay, with Spain sometimes visible across the water on clear days.

It is less packaged than the Spartel–caves loop. Come for air and perspective; facilities are limited compared with the city centre.

Good to know

FAQ about Cap Malabata

How far from the centre?

A short drive east of Tangier — typically under 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Better than Cap Spartel?

Different: Spartel is the classic lighthouse/caves outing; Malabata is quieter bay perspective.

Is there a lighthouse to visit?

The interest is the headland views; do not expect a full Spartel-style lighthouse stop.

Good in haze?

Long views suffer — prioritise clear days.

Family friendly?

Yes for a short viewpoint stop with supervision near edges.
