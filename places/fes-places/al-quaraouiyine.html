Al Quaraouiyine

A centre of learning founded in 859, still at the spiritual heart of the medina.

Best Time to GoEarly Morning
Time Needed30 – 60 Minutes
EntryExterior Only for Non-Muslims
WhereDeep in Fes el-Bali

Founded by a Woman in 859

Al Quaraouiyine was founded in 859 by Fatima al-Fihri, a woman from Kairouan who used her inheritance to build a mosque for the neighbourhood. Over the centuries it grew into a major centre of learning, and it is often described as one of the oldest continuously operating educational institutions in the world.

The complex is deep inside the medina and in active use, so non-Muslim visitors can't enter the prayer hall. You can still admire the exterior and the courtyard through the main doors, and take in the surrounding lanes. Access and the library can change, so ask locally, and dress and behave respectfully.

Practical Tips for Visiting Al Quaraouiyine

Getting There

It is in the heart of Fes el-Bali, reached on foot through the lanes from Bab Bou Jeloud in about 20 to 30 minutes. A guide helps.

Timing

Go in the morning when the lanes are calm, and avoid prayer times, especially on Friday.

Good to Know

Non-Muslims can't enter the prayer hall. Dress modestly, don't photograph worshippers and check locally for any visitor arrangements.

Good to Know

Al Quaraouiyine FAQ

Who founded Al Quaraouiyine?

Fatima al-Fihri founded it in 859, using her inheritance to build a mosque that grew into a great centre of learning.

Can tourists enter Al Quaraouiyine?

Non-Muslim visitors can't enter the prayer hall. You can view the exterior and glimpse the courtyard from the doors.

Can I visit the Al Quaraouiyine library?

Access to the library varies. Ask locally or through a guide about current visitor arrangements.

How long should I allow?

About 30 to 60 minutes, including a walk around the surrounding lanes.
