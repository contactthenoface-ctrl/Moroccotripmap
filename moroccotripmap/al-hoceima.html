Where the Rif mountains meet the Mediterranean — cliff-backed coves, golden urban beaches and a national park of sea and limestone cliffs.

Welcome to Al Hoceima — Mediterranean Rif, cliffs and clear water

Al Hoceima sits on Morocco’s northern Mediterranean coast, where the Rif mountains fall into turquoise coves and sandy bays. Limestone cliffs, fishing ports and a national park of land and sea define the place.

Most stays revolve around Quemado Beach in town, quieter coves like Cala Bonita, family sand at Sfiha, and outings into Al Hoceima National Park toward Cala Iris, the watchtowers of Torres de Alcalá and the wide sweep of Bades Bay.

Practical tips for Al Hoceima

Getting around

AHU airport transfers cover arrivals. Petits taxis handle town and nearby beaches; agree prices for park-coast day trips.

Sun & cliffs

Mediterranean sun is strong. High SPF, water and steady footwear on cliff paths in the national park.

Cash & season

Carry dirhams for taxis and small beach cafés. Book summer stays early; shoulder seasons are quieter and milder.

Good to know

Al Hoceima travel FAQ

How many days in Al Hoceima?

Three days cover Quemado, one or two nearby coves and a park-coast outing. Five days allow a slower pace plus Bades Bay and Torres de Alcalá.

Is Al Hoceima only for beach holidays?

Beaches are the main draw in summer, but the national park, cliff trails and fishing villages add walking and scenery beyond the sand.

How do I get to Al Hoceima?

Fly into Al Hoceima Airport (AHU) or arrive by road across the Rif. Domestic flights link from major Moroccan cities in season.

Best time to visit?

May to October for swimming and beach weather. Spring and autumn are better for hiking the park coast with less heat.
