Merzouga

Gateway to the Sahara and Morocco’s film cunes: Erg Chebbi, camel treks, desert camps and Saharan horizons — Merzouga is the door between the Atlas and the dunes.

Best Time to Visit Apr – Jun & Sep – Nov
Ideal Stay 2 – 3 Days
Top Vibe Desert & Film
Nearest Airport CMN (Mohammed V)

Welcome to Morocco’s White City

Merzouga sits on the edge of the High Atlas and the pre-Saharan plains. Kasbahs, film studios and the road to the desert define the town. It is less a medina city than a base for Erg Chebbi, camel treks and overnight desert trips toward Merzouga or Erfoud.

Two to three days cover Kasbah Tahebbi dunes, a camel trek and a night in camp — plus Khamlia, Dayet Srji or Rissani oasis if you stay longer. Use Merzouga as a stop on a Marrakech–desert circuit.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

Dunes, a night in camp, and the villages around Erg Chebbi.

Morning — Erg Chebbi viewpoint

Drive or walk to the edge of Erg Chebbi. Climb a dune for the first panoramic view before the heat peaks.

Afternoon — Merzouga village & fossils

Return to the village for lunch, fossil shops, and time to arrange your camel trek and camp.

Evening — Sunset on the dunes

Watch sunset from a dune ridge, then dinner at your hotel or a village restaurant.

Morning — Free or short walk

A quiet morning in the village or Hassilabied; rest before the afternoon trek.

Afternoon — Camel trek into Erg Chebbi

Depart as arranged with your camp: camel trek into the dunes toward your overnight stay.

Evening — Night in the Sahara

Berber dinner, drums and stars; sleep in a tent or camp with optional sunrise climb.

Morning — Sunrise & return

Climb for sunrise, breakfast at camp, then camel or 4x4 back to the village.

Afternoon — Khamlia or Rissani

Gnawa music and tea in Khamlia, or Rissani’s souk and Moulay Ali Cherif mausoleum.

Evening — Last night in Merzouga

Quiet dinner before onward travel toward Erfoud, Tinghir or Ouarzazate.

Practical Tips for Visiting Merzouga

Getting Around

The Casa Tramway is clean, cheap and useful for many central routes. For longer distances use red petit taxis (insist on the meter) or rideshare apps. The airport train is the easiest way from CMN.

Dress & Etiquette

Merzouga is more relaxed than inland cities, but modest clothing is still appreciated, especially when visiting the mosque. Bring a light jacket — the Atlantic breeze can be cool even in summer.

Safety & Atmosphere

Merzouga is a big city. Use normal big-city awareness, especially after dark in quieter streets. The Beach and main tourist areas feel lively and safe in the evening.

Day Trips

Best Day Trips from Merzouga

Easy train or car excursions that fit comfortably into a single day.

Erfoud & Draa Valley

South into the Draa Valley — palm groves, kasbahs and the road toward the desert.

Erfoud

UNESCO Portuguese citadel and the famous underground cistern, about 1h20 by train or car.

Dadès & Todra

Gorges and valley scenery on the road east — often combined with a desert circuit.

Marrakech (return)

Many travellers continue or return via the High Atlas Tizi n’Tichka pass to Marrakech.

Good to Know

Merzouga Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Merzouga?

Two full days cover the main sights comfortably. Three or four days allow a relaxed pace plus a day trip to Marrakech or Erfoud.

Is Merzouga safe for tourists?

Yes. Like any large city, use normal precautions. Tourist areas, the Beach and the centre are generally well-frequented and feel safe.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; keep some cash for taxis, markets and smaller shops.

How do I get from Mohammed V Airport to the city centre?

The ONCF airport train is the most convenient option (about 35–45 minutes). Official taxis and private transfers are also available; agree the price or use the meter.
