Akchour Waterfalls & God's Bridge

Emerald river pools, a natural rock arch and the best day hike near Chefchaouen.

Best Time to GoSpring & Early Autumn
Time NeededFull Day
EntryFree, Hike at Your Own Pace
WhereAbout 45 min from Chefchaouen

The Rif's Favourite Day Out

Akchour sits in the Talassemtane National Park, roughly 45 minutes by road from Chefchaouen. From the village a riverside trail follows the Oued Farda through gorges, past small cafés and swimming pools, to two highlights: the waterfalls and God's Bridge, a natural rock arch high above the valley.

Go prepared. The trail is uneven and there are stretches on rocks, so wear proper shoes, carry water and start early. The pools are cold and lovely in summer. Ask locally about trail conditions, since water levels and access change with the season and after rain. Many visitors hire a local guide.

Practical Tips for Visiting Akchour Waterfalls & God's Bridge

Getting There

From Chefchaouen take a grand taxi, a private driver or a tour (about 45 minutes). Agree the return time before you set off.

Timing

Spring and early autumn are best: warm, green and not crowded. Summer weekends can be very busy.

Good to Know

Bring water, sun protection and shoes with grip. Cash is useful for cafés and parking. Check local conditions after heavy rain.

Good to Know

Akchour Waterfalls & God's Bridge FAQ

How far is Akchour from Chefchaouen?

About 45 minutes by road, usually by grand taxi, private driver or organised tour.

Can you swim at Akchour?

Yes, in the river pools when conditions allow. The water is cold, so it is most pleasant in summer.

Do I need a guide for Akchour?

Not for the main trail, but a local guide helps with route, conditions and longer hikes.

What is the best time for the Akchour hike?

Spring for the greenery and flowing water, or early autumn for warm days without the summer crowds.
