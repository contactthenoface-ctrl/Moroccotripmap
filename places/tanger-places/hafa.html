Café Hafa

Cliff terraces and mint tea above the strait.

Best time
Late afternoon to sunset
Suggested duration
45 – 90 minutes
Entry
Free (consummation expected)
Location
Marshan cliffs, near kasbah

Tangier’s most famous tea view

Café Hafa has stacked terraces facing the Strait of Gibraltar. Writers and locals have used it for decades as a simple place to sit with mint tea and watch ships between Morocco and Spain.

It is about the view and the pause, not fine dining. Come for light and atmosphere; order tea or a soft drink and expect a shared, informal terrace culture.

What to order

Keep it simple: mint tea is the ritual. Soft drinks and basic items may be available; this is not a full restaurant.

  • Mint tea is the classic order.
  • Expect modest terrace service.
  • Cash is often easier.
  • Pair with a medina meal before or after.
  • Sunset seats fill fast.

Practical tips

Getting there

Walk from the kasbah area or take a petit taxi to Marshan / Café Hafa.

Seats

Terraces are stepped; arrive early for sunset.

Wind

Cliff-top breeze can be cool — a light layer helps.

Good to know

FAQ about Café Hafa

Do I have to order?

Yes — it is a working café. Tea is the usual minimum.

Is food served?

Focus is drinks and the view; do not expect a full menu.

Best time to come?

Late afternoon through sunset for the classic atmosphere.

How far from the medina?

A walk from the kasbah edge or a short taxi ride.

Is it touristy?

Popular with visitors and locals; the view still earns the stop.
