Cap Spartel

Lighthouse cliffs where two seas meet.

Best time
Clear mornings or late light
Suggested duration
45 – 90 minutes
Entry
Free outdoor area; lighthouse rules may apply
Location
≈20–30 min west of Tangier

The north-west corner of Africa’s Mediterranean edge

Cap Spartel marks the meeting zone of Atlantic and Mediterranean waters west of Tangier. A lighthouse stands on the headland; cliffs and wind define the stop more than a long walk network.

Most visitors combine it with the Hercules Caves in one half-day taxi loop. Bring a layer — exposure is real even when the city feels mild.

Practical tips

Getting there

Petit taxi or organised half-day with Hercules Caves; agree the round-trip price and waiting time.

Wind

Exposed headland — secure hats and light layers.

Time needed

A photo and walk stop, not a half-day hike.

Good to know

FAQ about Cap Spartel

How far from Tangier?

About 20–30 minutes by road west of the city.

Can I go inside the lighthouse?

Access rules vary; the outdoor viewpoint is the main visit for most travellers.

Combine with Hercules Caves?

Yes — the standard half-day loop from Tangier.

Is it worth it in bad weather?

Wind and fog reduce the experience; clear days are far better.

Entrance fee?

Outdoor areas are generally free; any lighthouse or parking fees are local and small.
