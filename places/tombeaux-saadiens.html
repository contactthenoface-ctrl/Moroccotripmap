History

Saadian Tombs

Richly decorated mausoleums with Carrara marble in the Kasbah district.

This royal necropolis dates back to the 16th-century reign of Sultan Ahmad al-Mansur and holds around 60 tombs of Saadian princes, along with over a hundred more graves belonging to family members and officials.

Sealed off and left forgotten for over two centuries, the site was rediscovered only in 1917 through an aerial survey. The main mausoleum's Hall of Twelve Columns, built with Italian Carrara marble, is the most celebrated room.

It's a compact site, so a visit of 30–45 minutes is usually enough, and it pairs well with nearby El Badi Palace and Bahia Palace in the Kasbah district.

Practical Info

  • Address: Rue de la Kasbah, near the Kasbah Mosque
  • Hours: Daily, roughly 9:00–17:00
  • Ticket: Around 70 MAD
  • Tip: Arrive close to opening time to avoid the small entrance passage getting crowded

Hours and prices can change without notice — check official sources before your visit.
