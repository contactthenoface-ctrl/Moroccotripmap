Grand Socco

The busy square between new town and medina.

Best time
Daytime; evenings lively
Suggested duration
30 – 60 minutes
Entry
Free
Location
Edge of medina & new town

Where Tangier switches gear

The Grand Socco — officially Place du 9 Avril 1947 — is the practical entrance to the medina. Taxis, buses of people and market energy meet here before the lanes tighten toward Petit Socco and the kasbah.

Use it as a landmark rather than a long stop: orient, enter the medina, return when you need a taxi. Nearby cinema and park edges add a bit of open space.

Good to know

FAQ about the Grand Socco

Is Grand Socco the same as Petit Socco?

No. Grand Socco is the large outer square; Petit Socco is a smaller café square deeper in the medina.

Can I leave luggage area taxis here?

It is a common drop-off to enter the medina on foot; confirm with your driver.

Is it worth a long stop?

Better as a gateway and meeting point than a half-day destination.

Safe at night?

Generally active; stick to lit routes into the medina.

Nearby landmarks?

Medina gates, cinema edge and the path toward Petit Socco.
