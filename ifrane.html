Ifrane

Morocco’s garden city in the Middle Atlas — cool air, cedar forests, lakes and ski slopes above 1,600 m.

Best Time to Go Spring, autumn; winter for snow
Ideal Stay 2 – 3 days
Vibe Alpine, clean, family-friendly
Region Middle Atlas · Ifrane Province

An alpine town that feels unlike the rest of Morocco

Ifrane sits high in the Middle Atlas at about 1,650 m, planned in the 1920s–30s as a garden city with pitched roofs, flower beds and wide avenues. Locals call it Little Switzerland — not for the Alps, but for the cool summers, winter snow and a pace that feels European next to Fez or Meknes.

You come for clean air, walks around Dayet Aoua, the cedar forest with Barbary macaques, Michlifen’s high plateau, and easy day links to Azrou. It is a base for nature more than monuments: lakes, forests and ski slopes when the season allows.

Plan

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

Town and lion first, lakes and cedars second, heights or park third.

Morning

Start at the stone lion and walk the clean avenues. Source Vittel park for a gentle introduction to the garden-city layout.

Afternoon

Al Akhawayn campus exterior if open to visitors. Coffee in the centre. Short valley walk toward Tizguit if energy remains.

Evening

Early dinner — Ifrane nights cool quickly. Rest for a full nature day tomorrow.

Morning

Drive or taxi to Dayet Aoua for lakeside walks and birdwatching. Soft light is best before midday.

Afternoon

Cedar forest for macaques and tall Atlas cedars. Keep distance from monkeys; no feeding.

Evening

Return to Ifrane. Simple grill or hotel dinner.

Morning

Michlifen for high views and, in season, ski or snow play. Or Dayet Hachlaf for a quieter water loop.

Afternoon

Ifrane National Park trail if you want more forest. Or continue toward Azrou and the cedar market.

Evening

Pack for Fez, Meknes or the Atlas route south.

Practical Tips for Ifrane

Pack layers

Even in July, evenings are cool. A light fleece saves the dinner walk.

Respect the macaques

Do not feed or chase monkeys in the cedar forest. Keep bags closed; they are skilled opportunists.

Car or organised day helps

Lakes and Michlifen are spread out. A driver for one day unlocks more than taxis alone.

FAQ

Ifrane FAQ

How many days in Ifrane?

Two days cover town, one lake and the cedars. A third day fits Michlifen or a second lake without rushing.

Is Ifrane worth visiting?

Yes if you want cool air, forests and a break from medina intensity — especially between Fez and the deeper Atlas.

Do I need a car?

Helpful. Town is walkable; lakes and Michlifen are easier with a driver or organised half-day.

Best season?

May–June and September–October for mild walks. December–February for snow if you want winter sports.
