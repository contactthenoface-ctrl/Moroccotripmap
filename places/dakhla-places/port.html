Fishing Port & Corniche

The working harbour and evening promenade — everyday Dakhla beyond the lagoon camps.

Best Time to Go Evening for the corniche
Time Needed 1 – 2 hours
Entry Free
Where Town centre

Town by the water

Dakhla’s port and corniche show the town’s fishing and daily rhythm. Boats, markets and a simple seafront walk give balance after lagoon days.

Evenings are for a slow stroll, cafés and fresh fish. The centre is low-rise and practical rather than monumental.

Use the port area as orientation when you first arrive, then branch out to lagoon camps or organised excursions.

Practical Tips for Visiting Fishing Port & Corniche

Getting There

Easily accessible by foot if staying in town, or by a short taxi ride from anywhere in Dakhla.

Photography

The bustling activity of fishermen and colorful boats make the port an incredible spot for morning and sunset photography.

Seafood Dining

Try the local grilled fish at nearby eateries where the day's catch is served fresh from the harbor.

Good to Know

Fishing Port & Corniche FAQ

Is the Dakhla Corniche free to visit?

Yes, walking along the corniche and viewing the harbor area is entirely free and open at all times.

What is the best time to visit the port and corniche?

Late afternoon and sunset offer the most vibrant atmosphere, golden light across the water, and active seaside promenades.

Can I buy fresh fish near the port?

Yes, the port area and nearby local markets bustle with fresh catches of the day brought in by local fishermen.

Are there restaurants along the corniche?

Several local cafés and seafood restaurants line the waterfront, offering fresh fish dishes and panoramic ocean views.

Is the area safe for evening walks?

Yes, the corniche is well-frequented by local families and visitors taking evening strolls, making it safe and welcoming.
