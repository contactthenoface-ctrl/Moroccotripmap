Place Outa el Hammam

The lively heart of Chefchaouen, surrounded by cafés, restaurants and the red-walled Kasbah.

LocationChefchaouen, Morocco
TypeMain Square
Good forExploring & Photography
About this place

Why visit Place Outa el Hammam?

The lively heart of Chefchaouen, surrounded by cafés, restaurants and the red-walled Kasbah.

A natural starting point for exploring the medina, with shaded terraces and a relaxed atmosphere.

MoroccoTripMap tip

Spend some time here before exploring the nearby blue lanes. The square is especially pleasant later in the afternoon when the medina begins to cool down.

Context

A little history

Chefchaouen’s central square sits at the meeting point of several important medina streets and the Kasbah. It remains one of the easiest places to orient yourself when arriving in the old town.

What to see and how to experience it

Take your time

Chefchaouen is best explored on foot, with time to notice details rather than rushing between landmarks.

Respect local life

The old town is also a residential neighborhood. Ask before photographing people and private homes.

Pair it with the medina

Combine this stop with nearby lanes, squares and viewpoints to make the most of your time in Chefchaouen.
