Ziz Valley, Cedar Forest & Fes Medina

An authentic off-the-beaten-path road trip for nature lovers and adventurers. Travel down Morocco's longest palm grove, explore ancient earthen Kasbahs, and drive 3 hours off-road to the wild, untouched dunes of Erg Chigaga.

Drive 3 hours off-road across hamada stone desert to wild Erg Chigaga dunes. Return west through Taliouine (Morocco's saffron capital) to walled Taroudant before returning to Marrakech or Agadir Airport.

Local Expert Tip: Unlike Merzouga, Erg Chigaga has no paved access roads. Always hire an experienced nomadic driver from M'Hamid who understands sand navigation and tire deflation techniques.
