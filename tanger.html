Tangier

Strait views, a layered medina and the meeting point of Atlantic and Mediterranean light.

Vibe Port city, strait winds and historic lanes
Access TNG Ibn Battuta · Al Boraq to Rabat/Casa · Ferry from Spain
Overview

Gateway city between two seas

Tangier sits where the Atlantic and the Mediterranean meet, with a medina that climbs toward the kasbah and a modern strip along the beach. It is a city of crossings — ferries from Spain, the Al Boraq train south, and day roads to Chefchaouen, Asilah and Tetouan.

Give yourself two or three days: medina and kasbah, Café Hafa on the cliffs, Cap Spartel and Hercules Caves to the west, and an easy seafront walk. Stay near the medina for atmosphere or on the corniche for beach access.

Sample plan

3 days in Tangier

A flexible outline — adjust for arrival time and day trips.

Morning — Medina & Petit Socco

Enter from Grand Socco, wander the lanes and pause at Petit Socco for coffee.

Afternoon — Kasbah Museum

Climb to the kasbah for the museum and terraces over the strait.

Evening — Café Hafa

Mint tea on the cliff terraces as the light softens over the water.

Morning — Cap Spartel

Taxi west to the lighthouse headland where Atlantic horizons open.

Afternoon — Hercules Caves

Short stop at the sea caves and the famous opening toward the ocean.

Evening — Medina dinner

Back in town for a medina restaurant or a quieter new-town option.

Morning — Tangier Beach or Cap Malabata

Easy morning on the city beach / corniche, or east to Cap Malabata for open views.

Afternoon — American Legation or free time

Visit the American Legation museum in the medina, or keep time for shopping and cafés.

Evening — Departure options

Train or road links toward Rabat, Chefchaouen or the airport depending on your route.

Food

Where to eat in Tangier

Medina kitchens, port seafood and café terraces — from Petit Socco pauses to corniche dinners.

  • Petit Socco cafés for a classic medina break
  • Seafood near the port and lower medina
  • Corniche and hotel restaurants on the beach strip
  • Simple grills and juice stands for quick meals
  • Cash remains useful for smaller places

Practical tips for Tangier

Getting around

Petit taxis for short hops; agree meter or price. Walking works well in the medina and along the corniche.

Airport & train

TNG Ibn Battuta airport serves the city. Al Boraq high-speed trains link Tangier with Rabat and Casablanca.

Day trips

Chefchaouen, Asilah and Tetouan are the usual road outings; Cap Spartel is a half-day west of town.

Beyond the city

Day trips from Tangier

Chefchaouen

Blue medina in the Rif — about 2–2.5 hours by road. Full day recommended.

Asilah

Atlantic medina and ramparts south of Tangier — roughly 1 hour. Ideal half-day or easy day.

Tetouan

UNESCO medina and Andalusian heritage — about 1 hour toward the east.

Good to know

FAQ about Tangier

How many days in Tangier?

Two full days cover the medina, kasbah, Café Hafa and Cap Spartel. A third day suits a beach morning or a trip to Asilah or Chefchaouen.

Where should I stay?

Medina and kasbah for atmosphere and walking; corniche / beach for modern hotels and sea access.

Is Tangier a good base for Chefchaouen?

Yes as a day trip (long day) or overnight. Many travellers continue south after Tangier via Chefchaouen.

Airport code and transfers?

Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport (TNG). Petit taxis and pre-booked transfers cover the ride into town.
