Souss Valley

Taroudant

Ochre ramparts, authentic souks and palm oases: a relaxed town of the Souss valley.

Overview

Taroudant is a walled town in the Souss valley, between the High Atlas and the Anti-Atlas. It can be visited in one or two days, on foot or by horse-drawn calèche, between ramparts, souks and lively squares.

Best time

Spring and autumn

Suggested duration

1 to 2 days

Getting there

About 80 km from Agadir

Practical tips

  • Start early: the streets are cooler and the souks calmer.
  • Rent a calèche or a bike to go around the ramparts.
  • Taroudant combines well with Agadir or Tiznit as a day trip.

Frequently asked questions

How long do you need in Taroudant?

One day covers the essentials; two days let you add the Tiout oasis.

How do you get there from Agadir?

By shared taxi, bus or car; allow about an hour each way.

When is the best time to go?

Spring and autumn; summer is very hot.
