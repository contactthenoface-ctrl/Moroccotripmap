Source Tizguit

Spring and green valley near Ifrane — a short nature escape from town.

Best time Morning or late afternoon
Suggested time 1 – 2 hours
Entry Free access
Location Near Ifrane

Water and green near town

Source Tizguit is a local nature stop: spring water, greenery and a break from the main road. It suits travellers who want a simple outdoor hour without driving far into the mountains.

Wear shoes with grip if paths are wet. Bring water even though you are near a spring — do not assume potable fountains.

Eating near Source Tizguit

Limited or no services on site — plan food in Ifrane before or after.

  • Eat in Ifrane town.
  • Carry drinking water.
  • Small snacks for the walk.
  • No reliable restaurants on site.
  • Hotel or café dinner back in Ifrane.

Picnic only if you pack out all waste. Do not assume spring water is treated.
