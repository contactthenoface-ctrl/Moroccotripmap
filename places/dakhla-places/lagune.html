Dakhla Lagoon

A 40 km turquoise lagoon between Sahara dunes and the Atlantic — the geographic and sporting heart of Dakhla.

Best Time to Go Morning & late afternoon
Time Needed Half day to full day
Entry Free (activities extra)
Where Peninsula lagoon

The lagoon that defines Dakhla

Dakhla’s lagoon stretches for roughly forty kilometres along a sand peninsula. Sheltered from open-ocean swell, its shallow turquoise water stays remarkably flat — the reason the world comes here to kitesurf and wingfoil.

Even without a board, the lagoon is the main stage: boat trips, oyster farms, flamingos in season, and light that shifts from silver to deep blue across the day.

Most camps and schools sit on the lagoon shore. Town is a short drive; the White Dune and Dragon Island are classic lagoon outings.

Practical Tips for Visiting Dakhla Lagoon

Getting Around

Taxis or rental cars are needed to reach the various lagoon camps and spots down the peninsula from Dakhla town.

Sun & Wind Protection

The combination of desert sun and steady coastal wind means high SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, and a windbreaker are essential.

Tides & Nature

Be mindful of changing tides when walking on the flats, and respect local wildlife areas and protected bird habitats.

Good to Know

Dakhla Lagoon FAQ

Is Dakhla Lagoon free to visit?

Yes, accessing the shores of the lagoon is completely free. Costs apply only if you rent water sports equipment, book boat excursions, or dine at the oyster farms and camps.

What is the best time to visit the lagoon?

Early morning offers the calmest glass-like water and stunning sunrise colors, while late afternoon provides magical golden light reflecting across the tidal flats.

Can I see wildlife at Dakhla Lagoon?

Yes! The lagoon is a protected Ramsar site and a crucial stop for migratory birds, including large flocks of flamingos, herons, and waders.

What are the local culinary specialties here?

Dakhla is famous for its fresh local oysters farmed directly in the clean waters of the lagoon, as well as fresh Atlantic fish and traditional Saharan mint tea.

How far is the lagoon from Dakhla town?

The main kite camps and central areas of the lagoon are located along the peninsula, roughly a 20 to 30-minute drive from downtown Dakhla.
