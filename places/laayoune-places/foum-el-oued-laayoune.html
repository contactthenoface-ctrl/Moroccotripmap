Foum El Oued Beach

A sweeping stretch of Atlantic coastline near Laâyoune, offering refreshing ocean breezes, endless sandy shores, and magnificent sunset views.

Best Time to Go Late Afternoon – Sunset
Time Needed 2 – 4 Hours
Entry Free, Open 24/7
Where 20 km West of Laâyoune

Where the Sahara Meets the Atlantic Ocean

Foum El Oued Beach is the premier coastal getaway for residents and visitors of Laâyoune. Located just a short drive west of the city center where the desert dunes gently slope down to meet the powerful waves of the Atlantic, it provides a breathtaking contrast of arid landscapes and marine expanses.

The coastline features wide, untouched sandy beaches that stretch as far as the eye can see. It is a favorite destination for those looking to escape the inland heat, enjoy the crisp ocean air, and witness dramatic sunsets over the horizon.

Whether you plan to take a peaceful stroll along the shoreline, breathe in the iodine-rich sea breeze, or relax at a nearby café overlooking the water, Foum El Oued offers a refreshing breath of coastal tranquility.

Coastal Dining & Fresh Seafood

Being right on the Atlantic seaboard, Foum El Oued and its surrounding route are ideal for sampling the ocean's bounty. Local establishments serve fresh catches brought straight from the nearby waters.

  • Grilled Sardines and local white fish, prepared simply with olive oil, garlic, and cumin.
  • Seafood Tagines cooked with fresh tomatoes, peppers, and aromatic Moroccan spices.
  • Refreshing mint tea served at ocean-view terraces while listening to the waves.
  • Fresh local flatbreads baked daily in traditional ovens.

Dining near the beach as the sun goes down provides a wonderfully peaceful and authentic coastal dining experience.

Practical Tips for Visiting Foum El Oued Beach

Getting There

Foum El Oued is situated 20 km west of Laâyoune. Grand taxis or private cars make the scenic 20-minute drive down the coastal road regularly.

Weather & Attire

Ocean winds can be strong and cool, even on warm days. Bringing a windbreaker, light jacket, or sweater is highly recommended for beach walks.

Photography

The vast expanses where golden dunes meet the blue Atlantic create stunning photographic opportunities, especially during golden hour before sunset.

Good to Know

Foum El Oued Beach FAQ

Is Foum El Oued Beach free to visit?

Yes, Foum El Oued Beach is a public coastal area accessible freely 24/7.

How far is Foum El Oued from central Laâyoune?

The beach is located about 20 kilometers west of central Laâyoune, easily reachable by grand taxi or car in roughly 20 minutes.

What is the best time to visit Foum El Oued Beach?

Late afternoon is ideal to enjoy the refreshing Atlantic breeze and watch the spectacular sunset over the ocean.

Are there amenities near the beach?

Yes, the surrounding seaside area features local cafés, small eateries, and spaces to relax by the shore.
