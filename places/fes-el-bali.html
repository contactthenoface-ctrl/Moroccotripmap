UNESCO Medina

Fes el-Bali

Fes el-Bali is the historic core of Fes and one of the most rewarding places in Morocco to explore on foot. Its narrow lanes lead past workshops, small shops, historic buildings and quiet residential corners, creating a city that still feels lived in rather than preserved behind glass.

The best way to experience the medina is slowly. Follow the flow of the streets, pause for mint tea, watch artisans at work and allow time for the architecture to reveal itself around each corner.

The medina is especially known for its traditional crafts, including leather, metalwork, woodwork, textiles and ceramics. Several of Fes’s major monuments and historic quarters are concentrated within or around its maze of streets.

Because many lanes are pedestrian-only and the layout can be confusing, a licensed local guide can be useful for a first visit. Comfortable shoes and a little patience make a big difference.
