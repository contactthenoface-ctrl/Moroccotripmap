Café Hafa

Terraced cliff café in Marshan — mint tea and long views over the Strait of Gibraltar.

Best time Late afternoon to sunset
Suggested duration 1 – 2 hours
Entry Order a drink
Location Marshan cliffs
Overview

Tea above the strait

Café Hafa has been a Tangier ritual for generations: simple terraces stepped down the cliff, mint tea, and the Strait of Gibraltar stretching toward Spain. It is about the view and the pause, not a full restaurant menu.

Walk from the kasbah area or take a petit taxi to Marshan. Arrive early for sunset seats. Pair with the Kasbah Museum for a strong afternoon on the heights.

Good to know

FAQ about Café Hafa

Must I order?

Yes — it is a working café. Tea is the usual minimum.

Do they serve food?

Focus is drinks and the view; do not expect a full menu.

Best time?

Late afternoon through sunset for the classic atmosphere.

Far from the medina?

A walk from the kasbah edge or a short taxi.

Is it touristy?

Popular with visitors and locals; the view still justifies the stop.
