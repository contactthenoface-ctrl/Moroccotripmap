Ifrane: White Medina, town centre and the Road to the Sea

Ifrane is the white city of Andalusian memory: monumental gates, vast granaries and a medina that is easier to walk than Fez. town centre and town centre set the scene; the kasbah shows the scale of the sultan’s logistics. It is also the natural base for Dayet Aoua and Azrou Zerhoun without the day-trip rush from farther cities.

Two full days cover the imperial core and the medina; a third day fits the Roman ruins and the holy hillside town. Stays cluster near the medina and Ville Nouvelle — practical if you arrive by train and want both history and calm evenings.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A relaxed schedule to see the highlights without rushing.

Morning — Stone lion & centre

Start at the stone lion and walk the clean garden-city avenues; Source Vittel park shows the town plan.

Afternoon — Al Akhawayn exterior

See the campus edge if accessible, then coffee in the centre. Short valley walk toward Tizguit if energy remains.

Evening — Early dinner

Dine early — Ifrane nights cool fast even in July.

Morning — Dayet Aoua

Drive or taxi to Dayet Aoua for shore walks and birds. Morning light is best.

Afternoon — Cedar forest

Continue to the cedar stands for Barbary macaques. Do not feed the monkeys; close bags.

Evening — Back in town

Simple grill or hotel dinner after the forest day.

Morning — Michlifen or Dayet Hachlaf

Michlifen for high views (ski in season) or the quieter Dayet Hachlaf loop.

Afternoon — National park trail or Azrou

A short park path, or continue toward Azrou market if you leave the plateau.

Evening — Departure

Bags for Fez, Meknes or deeper Atlas routes.

Practical Tips for Visiting Ifrane

Getting around without wasting time

The town centre is walkable. Lakes, Michlifen and the cedar forest need a driver or organised half-day — taxis alone eat time and budget.

Shopping and expectations

Ifrane is not a souk city. Buy simple supplies in the centre; cedar crafts are easier in Azrou market if you continue that way.

Layers, altitude and timing

Even in summer, evenings are cool at 1,650 m. Pack a jacket. Start lake and forest walks in the morning; avoid feeding macaques.

Good to Know

Ifrane Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Ifrane?

Two full days cover the main sights comfortably. Three or four days allow a relaxed pace plus a day trip to Marrakech or Salé.

Is Ifrane safe for tourists?

Yes. Like any large city, use normal precautions. Tourist areas, the Beach and the centre are generally well-frequented and feel safe.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; keep some cash for taxis, markets and smaller shops.

How do I get to Ifrane from Fes or the airport?

The ONCF airport train is the most convenient option (about 35–45 minutes). Official taxis and private transfers are also available; agree the price or use the meter.
