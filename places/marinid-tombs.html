Marinid Tombs Viewpoint

The Marinid Tombs viewpoint is one of the classic places to step back from the tight streets of Fes and see the city as a whole. From the hill above the medina, the rooftops and minarets spread across the landscape below.

The ruins associated with the Marinid tombs sit on a hillside outside the old city, while the main attraction for many visitors is the broad panorama over Fes el-Bali and the surrounding hills.

Sunset is popular, but the viewpoint can also be rewarding earlier in the day when visibility is clearer. Bring water and take care on uneven ground around the ruins.

The site is best combined with a taxi ride rather than treated as a long uphill walk from the medina. Allow enough time to return before dark if you are exploring independently.
