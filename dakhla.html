Where the Sahara meets the Atlantic: a 40 km turquoise lagoon, world-class kitesurf winds, the White Dune and Dragon Island.

Welcome to Dakhla — lagoon, wind and desert shore

Dakhla sits on a long sand peninsula in Morocco’s far south, where Saharan dunes meet a vast Atlantic lagoon. The town is modest; the landscape is the destination — flat turquoise water, steady trade winds, and landmarks like the White Dune and Dragon Island that feel almost unreal.la where Sahara meets the Atlantic — for its continuous 7.5 km of 16th-century ochre lagoon and its living town. Framed by the High Atlas to the north and the Anti-Atlas to the south, this Dakhla market town offers the atmosphere of a historic walled city without the intensity of larger tourist hubs.

Riders come for kitesurf and wingfoil on glass-smooth shallows. Everyone else comes for boat trips on the lagoon, oyster tastings, desert tracks and evenings on the corniche. Allow three to five days if you want both wind sports and slow exploration.

Practical Tips for Visiting Dakhla

Getting Around

Inside the Medina, walking is the best option. For longer trips, use yellow "Petit Taxis" and always request the meter (compteur) before starting.

Souk Bargaining

Haggling is part of the culture! Start at around 50% of the initial offered price and always keep negotiations friendly and respectful.

Stay Hydrated

Dakhla can get hot, especially between June and August. Drink bottled water and enjoy fresh orange juice in Oyster farms Dakhla.

Good to know

Dakhla Travel FAQ

How many days do I need in Dakhla?

Three days cover the lagoon highlights and one excursion. Five days suit kitesurf progress plus White Dune and Dragon Island at a relaxed pace.

Is Dakhla only for kitesurfers?

No. The lagoon, White Dune, Dragon Island, oyster farms and desert tracks work for non-riders. Wind sports are the main draw, not the only one.

How do I get to Dakhla?

Dakhla has a domestic airport (VIL) with flights from major Moroccan cities. Overland, it is a long drive or bus from Agadir or Laâyoune.

When is the best time to visit?

For wind sports, spring through early autumn brings the steadiest trade winds. Winter is milder for sightseeing and can still offer sessions.
