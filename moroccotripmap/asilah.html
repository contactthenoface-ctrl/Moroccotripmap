Welcome to Asilah — Atlantic breeze, whitewashed walls, and street art

Perched on northern Morocco's Atlantic coast, Asilah is a peaceful enclave known for its pristine whitewashed medina, vibrant murals painted by international artists, and dramatic sea-facing Portuguese ramparts.

Whether you visit for the annual Cultural Moussem, wander quiet blue-accented alleyways, enjoy freshly caught grilled fish by the harbour, or head south to the golden expanse of Paradise Beach, Asilah offers an effortless coastal retreat.

Planning

Suggested 2-day plan for Asilah

Art exploration, rampart sunsets, and ocean relaxation.

Morning — Medina Murals & Alleyways

Enter via Bab Al-Kasbah, photograph painted murals, and explore quiet whitewashed streets.

Afternoon — Palace & Monuments

Visit Raissouni Palace (if open during events) and pass by Borj Al-Kamra tower.

Evening — Borj Krikia Sunset

Gather at Borj Krikia on the ramparts for sunset over the Atlantic, followed by seafood near the port.

Morning — Excursion to Paradise Beach

Take a taxi or horse carriage south to Paradise Beach (Rmilat) for a relaxing coastal morning.

Afternoon — Beach Relaxation or Surfing

Enjoy tea in seasonal beach shacks or walk along the cliff-backed golden sands.

Evening — Corniche Walk

Return to town for a final evening stroll along the lively beachside corniche.

Practical tips for Asilah

Getting around

The medina is entirely pedestrian. Petits taxis or horse carriages are easy to hire for trips to Paradise Beach.

Art & Moussem

The annual Arts Festival takes place in July/August, bringing fresh murals and cultural concerts.

Atmosphere

Asilah is very quiet compared to Tangier. Perfect for relaxing, photography, and ocean sunsets.

Good to know

Asilah travel FAQ

How many days should I stay in Asilah?

One or two days are perfect for exploring the medina, enjoying seafood, watching Atlantic sunsets, and spending an afternoon at Paradise Beach.

When is the Asilah Arts Festival held?

The International Cultural Moussem of Asilah is held annually in mid-summer (usually July or August), attracting international artists who repaint the medina wall murals.

How do I get from Tangier to Asilah?

Asilah is 45 km south of Tangier. You can take a direct ONCF train (30 mins), a grand taxi, or drive via the A1 highway.

Is Asilah good for beach lovers?

Yes, Asilah has long sandy beaches extending both north and south, with Paradise Beach (Rmilat) being one of the most stunning coastal spots in northern Morocco.
