Tetouan

White Andalusian medina under the Rif — crafts, museums and the Mediterranean nearby.

Best Time to Go Spring & autumn; mild winters
Ideal Stay 2 – 3 days
Vibe Authentic, walkable, less crowded than Fez
Region Northern Morocco · Rif foothills

Tetouan: White Medina, Place Hassan II and the Road to the Sea

Tetouan is the white city of Andalusian memory: monumental gates, vast granaries and a medina that is easier to walk than Fez. Place Hassan II and Place Hassan II set the scene; the kasbah shows the scale of the sultan’s logistics. It is also the natural base for Martil and Chefchaouen Zerhoun without the day-trip rush from farther cities.

Two full days cover the imperial core and the medina; a third day fits the Roman ruins and the holy hillside town. Stays cluster near the medina and Ville Nouvelle — practical if you arrive by train and want both history and calm evenings.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A relaxed schedule to see the highlights without rushing.

Morning

Enter the medina from Place Hassan II. Walk the main souk axes without rushing — leather, textiles, and food lanes. Climb toward the kasbah for the first elevated view.

Afternoon

Archaeological Museum if open, then a slow loop back through quieter residential lanes. Tea on or near the square.

Evening

Dinner in the medina or the Spanish quarter. Early night if you plan Martil tomorrow.

Morning

Modern Art Museum and any temporary shows. Walk the royal palace exterior and the cleaner avenues of the new town.

Afternoon

Artisan quarter and workshops — ask before photographing. Mellah lanes if you want a quieter historic pocket.

Evening

Sunset viewpoint above the medina if the light is clear. Simple grill dinner.

Morning

Taxi or local bus to Martil for beach time and seafood. Or start toward Chefchaouen if you are continuing south into the Rif.

Afternoon

Swim, walk the corniche, or return to Tetouan for last shopping and a museum you missed.

Evening

Pack and connect to Tangier, Chefchaouen, or the coast.

Practical Tips for Tetouan

Walk the medina with a reset point

Use Place Hassan II as your compass. When lanes confuse you, return to the square instead of accepting the first shortcut offer.

Combine city and sea

Martil is close enough for an afternoon. Do the medina and museums first so the beach feels like a reward, not a substitute.

Spanish still helps

Tetouan’s history means Spanish is widely understood alongside Arabic and French — useful in shops and taxis.

Good to Know

Tetouan Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Tetouan?

Two full days cover the main sights comfortably. Three or four days allow a relaxed pace plus a day trip to Marrakech or Salé.

Is Tetouan safe for tourists?

Yes. Like any large city, use normal precautions. Tourist areas, the Beach and the centre are generally well-frequented and feel safe.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; keep some cash for taxis, markets and smaller shops.

How do I get to Tetouan from Tangier or the airport?

The ONCF airport train is the most convenient option (about 35–45 minutes). Official taxis and private transfers are also available; agree the price or use the meter.
