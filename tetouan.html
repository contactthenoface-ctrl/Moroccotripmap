Tetouan: White Medina, Place Hassan II and the Road to the Sea

Tetouan is the white city of Andalusian memory: monumental gates, vast granaries and a medina that is easier to walk than Fez. Place Hassan II and Place Hassan II set the scene; the kasbah shows the scale of the sultan’s logistics. It is also the natural base for Martil and Chefchaouen Zerhoun without the day-trip rush from farther cities.

Two full days cover the imperial core and the medina; a third day fits the Roman ruins and the holy hillside town. Stays cluster near the medina and Ville Nouvelle — practical if you arrive by train and want both history and calm evenings.

Planning

Suggested 3-Day Itinerary

A relaxed schedule to see the highlights without rushing.

Morning — Place Hassan II & Place Hassan II

Start in the Kasbah lanes and Andalusian Gardens while the light is soft. Climb to the viewpoint over the Zerhoun estuary.

Afternoon — Medina & Corniche

Walk the compact medina, then continue down to the beach and corniche for Atlantic air and a café stop.

Evening — Marina or centre dinner

Head to the Zerhoun marina for a modern waterfront stroll, or dine in the centre / Agdal.

Morning — the kasbah & Mausoleum

Visit the the kasbah esplanade and the the Royal Palace exterior. Dress modestly for the mausoleum interior.

Afternoon — Martil or museums

Taxi to Martil for beach time and seafood by the Mediterranean — one of Tetouan’s quietest major sites.

Evening — Soft city night

Return to the centre for a calm dinner; Tetouan evenings are lower-key than Marrakech or Casablanca.

Morning — Musée RoyalI or free time

If open, visit the modern art museum, or revisit a favourite corner of the Kasbah without a fixed plan.

Afternoon — Salé or Casablanca

Cross to Salé for a short contrast, or take the train to Casablanca for the Hassan II Mosque if time allows.

Evening — Departure or last walk

A final corniche or marina walk before continuing your trip north or south.

Practical Tips for Visiting Tetouan

Getting around without wasting time

The medina and Place Hassan II are walkable. Use petits taxis for the kasbah and the mausoleum if heat is high. For Martil, a petit taxi is enough; for Chefchaouen, plan a full day or join a small tour so you are not stuck waiting for returns. The train station sits in Ville Nouvelle — budget a short taxi to the medina on arrival.

Shopping and expectations

Souks are calmer than Fez. Polite bargaining is normal for crafts, spices and olives. Fixed-price museum tickets and official sites leave little room for negotiation — keep energy for the medina stalls instead.

Heat, water and timing

Summer on the plateau is harsh at midday. Do the medina and kasbah in the morning, carry water, and use the medina’s shade for afternoons. Spring and autumn give the most comfortable light on Place Hassan II and the Roman stones.

Good to Know

Tetouan Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Tetouan?

Two full days cover the main sights comfortably. Three or four days allow a relaxed pace plus a day trip to Marrakech or Salé.

Is Tetouan safe for tourists?

Yes. Like any large city, use normal precautions. Tourist areas, the Beach and the centre are generally well-frequented and feel safe.

What currency is used and can I pay by card?

Morocco uses the dirham (MAD). Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants; keep some cash for taxis, markets and smaller shops.

How do I get to Tetouan from Tangier or the airport?

The ONCF airport train is the most convenient option (about 35–45 minutes). Official taxis and private transfers are also available; agree the price or use the meter.
