Grand Socco

The open square that links Tangier’s new town to the medina gates.

Best time Anytime as a meeting point
Suggested duration 30 – 60 minutes
Entry Free public square
Location Medina edge / city centre
Overview

Where the new town meets the old

Grand Socco is the practical heart of Tangier’s visitor map: buses and taxis, café terraces, and the main pedestrian gates into the medina. It is more junction than monument — useful, lively and easy to find.

Use it as a drop-off, a regroup point or the start of a medina walk toward Petit Socco and the kasbah. Cinema and market edges add local colour without requiring a long stop.

Good to know

FAQ about Grand Socco

Grand Socco and Petit Socco same place?

No. Grand Socco is the large outer square; Petit Socco is a smaller café square deeper in the medina.

Can I be dropped with luggage?

Yes — a common drop for walking into the medina; confirm with your driver.

Worth a long stop?

Better as a gateway and meeting point than a half-day visit.

Safe at night?

Generally busy; stick to lit routes toward the medina.

Nearby landmarks?

Medina gates, cinema edge and the path toward Petit Socco.
