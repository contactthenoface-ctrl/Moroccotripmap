American Legation

Historic museum and courtyard in the medina — first American public property abroad.

Best time Morning for quieter rooms
Suggested duration 45 – 90 minutes
Entry Paid museum ticket
Location Inside Tangier medina

History in the medina lanes

The American Legation sits inside Tangier medina as a museum and cultural centre with a calm courtyard and rooms away from the busier alleys.

Check opening days before you go. Easy to combine with Petit Socco and a kasbah climb on the same medina day.

Eat near the Legation

The museum sits inside the medina, so cafés at Petit Socco and simple restaurants on the surrounding lanes are the practical options before or after your visit.

  • Petit Socco for a classic café pause.
  • Simple Moroccan plates in nearby medina restaurants.
  • Tea and pastry for a light stop.
  • Cash useful for small places.
  • New-town dining if you want more variety after leaving the medina.

Plan the Legation as a culture stop, then eat in the medina or new town on the same loop.

Good to know

FAQ about American Legation

Still a diplomatic office?

It operates today as a museum and cultural centre, with deep historical ties between the US and Morocco.

Ticket required?

Yes — paid museum entry.

How long to spend?

Forty-five to ninety minutes suits most visitors.

Hard to find?

Medina navigation helps; save the location offline or ask on site.

Interesting without history passion?

The courtyard and setting alone offer a peaceful contrast to busier lanes.
