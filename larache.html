Larache

Atlantic northwest — Lixus ruins, medina and coastal calm near Asilah.

Best time April – June & September – October · mild coastal days
Ideal stay 1 – 2 nights
Atmosphere Lixus · Medina · Atlantic calm
Access Tangier (TNG) ≈ 1.5h · Asilah ≈ 40 min · Rabat ≈ 2.5–3h

Welcome to Larache — Lixus and the Atlantic

Larache sits on Morocco's northwestern Atlantic coast at the mouth of the Loukkos River. It is quieter than Tangier or Asilah, with a compact medina, Spanish-era streets and one of the region's most important ancient sites: Lixus.

Plan one or two nights for the ruins, a waterfront walk and a slower coastal pace. It combines easily with Asilah or as a stop between Tangier and Rabat.

Archaeology

Lixus archaeological site

Lixus is Larache's headline stop: a hilltop site with Phoenician roots and major Roman remains, overlooking the Loukkos estuary. It is quieter than Volubilis and easy to combine with a Larache stay.

Allow time to walk the ruins and the views. Wear sturdy shoes, bring water and check opening hours locally.

Morning visits are cooler and often less crowded.

Medina

Medina of Larache

Larache's medina is compact and lived-in — lanes, small shops and a slower rhythm than the big imperial cities.

Go in the morning for softer light. Dress modestly and ask before close-up photos of people.

Pair with a café stop near the waterfront.

Coast

Waterfront & port

The Atlantic edge and port area give Larache its coastal character — fishing boats, sea air and an easy evening stroll.

Sunset light is often soft along the water. Keep valuables secure in busier spots.

Good place for a simple fish plate when the catch is in.

Heritage

Spanish-era streets

Larache retains a clear Spanish protectorate imprint: plazas, administrative buildings and a street grid that contrasts with the medina.

A short walk is enough to sense the dual character of the town — Moroccan medina and colonial-era centre.

Best combined with the medina the same morning.

Beach

Beach & dunes

Sandy Atlantic beaches near Larache offer open space and strong coastal light. Conditions depend on wind and swell.

Swim only where it looks safe; Atlantic currents can be strong. Bring sun protection.

Late afternoon is pleasant for a walk when the heat eases.

Nature

Loukkos estuary

The Loukkos meets the Atlantic at Larache, creating wetlands and a green corridor that frames Lixus and the coast.

Birdlife and open views reward a short outing; respect private land and fragile banks.

Pairs naturally with a Lixus visit.

Practical tips for Larache

Getting around

Centre and medina on foot. Taxi or car for Lixus; Asilah and Tangier are easy road links.

Atlantic conditions

Wind and swell change quickly. Check local advice before swimming.

When to come

Spring and autumn are mild. Summer is busy on the coast; winter is quieter and can be rainy.

Planning

Suggested 2–3 Day Itinerary

A balanced plan for Lixus, the medina and Atlantic air without rushing.

Morning — Lixus archaeological site

Head to Lixus while the light is soft. Walk the ruins and take in the Loukkos views.

Afternoon — Medina of Larache

Explore the compact medina and Spanish-era streets at a slow pace.

Evening — Waterfront

Stroll the port edge and eat a simple fish or grill plate.

Overnight in Asilah, Tangier or further along the coast depending on your route.

Good to know

Questions about Larache

How long should I stay in Larache?

One night covers Lixus and the centre; two nights if you want a slower coastal pace and Asilah.

What is Lixus?

An important Phoenician and Roman archaeological site on a hill near Larache, overlooking the Loukkos estuary.

How do I get to Larache?

By road from Tangier (about 1.5h), Asilah (about 40 min) or Rabat (about 2.5–3h). Grands taxis and buses serve the coast.

Is Larache quieter than Asilah?

Yes — Larache is less polished and less crowded, with a stronger everyday port-town feel.
