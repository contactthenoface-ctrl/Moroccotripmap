Nador

Where the Rif meets a vast Mediterranean lagoon: Marchica, a lively corniche, mountain viewpoints and wild headlands toward Cap des Trois Fourches.

Best time May – October for beaches; spring & autumn for hiking and views
Ideal stay 3 – 5 days
Vibe Lagoon, corniche, Rif coast, ferry gateway
Airport / port Nador (NDR) · Beni Ansar ferry

Welcome to Nador — lagoon light and Rif coast

Nador sits on Morocco’s northeastern Mediterranean shore, defined less by an old town than by water: the Marchica lagoon (Mar Chica), one of the largest saltwater lagoons in the Mediterranean basin, stretches for roughly twenty-five kilometres beside the city.la where Rif meets the Mediterranean — for its vast turquoise bay and desert-meets-ocean horizon. Framed by the High Atlas to the north and the Anti-Atlas to the south, this Nador market town offers the atmosphere of a historic walled city without the intensity of larger tourist hubs.

Evenings belong to the corniche. Day trips pull you toward Mount Gourougou, the dramatic Cap des Trois Fourches peninsula, quieter beaches like Charrana and Ras El Ma, and the working port of Beni Ansar. Melilla lies just across the border for a short passport day if you want the contrast.

Practical tips for Nador

Getting around

Town and corniche are walkable; beaches and Cap des Trois Fourches need a car or organised transfer. Beni Ansar is a short hop for ferries. Agree taxi prices clearly.

Sun & seasons

Mediterranean sun is strong in summer. High SPF and water for lagoon and beach days. Spring and autumn are ideal for Gourougou and the Cap.

Cash & border

Carry dirhams for taxis and small stops. If you day-trip to Melilla, bring a valid passport and check border hours.

Good to know

Nador travel FAQ

How many days in Nador?

Three days cover the lagoon, corniche and one mountain or beach outing. Five days suit Cap des Trois Fourches, Charrana and a slower rhythm without rushing.

Is Nador only the lagoon?

Marchica is the signature landscape, but Gourougou, Cap des Trois Fourches, nearby beaches and Beni Ansar add mountain, wild coast and working-port energy.

How do I get to Nador?

Flights to NDR, ferries via Beni Ansar, or road links along the northeastern Rif. Melilla is adjacent for some cross-border itineraries.

Best time to visit?

May to October for swimming and corniche evenings. Spring and autumn are excellent for hiking and clearer long views.
