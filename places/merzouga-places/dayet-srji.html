The Seasonal Lake of Merzouga

Seasonal lake that can draw flamingos after rains. Dayet Srji rewards a slow visit: look at the water reflections, migratory birds and how wildlife thrives on the edge of the desert.

Water levels vary depending on recent rainfall. Confirm conditions locally on arrival and keep plans flexible around daytime heat.

Pair Dayet Srji with camel treks or dune excursions nearby to round out your Merzouga desert experience.

Practical Tips for Visiting Dayet Srji

Seasonal Water

Check locally before visiting to confirm if water and flamingos are present after seasonal rains.

4x4 or Quad

Accessing the lake tracks is easiest with a 4x4 vehicle or local guide.

Sun Protection

The open lake area offers little shade — bring sunglasses, a hat and sunscreen.

Good to Know

Dayet Srji FAQ

How long do I need at Dayet Srji?

Most visitors spend between 30 to 45 minutes observing the lake and birdlife.

Is there an entrance fee for Dayet Srji?

No, Dayet Srji is a natural seasonal wetland and is free to visit.

When is the best time to visit Dayet Srji?

Mornings are cooler and ideal for birdwatching, while late afternoons offer wonderful warm light.

Will I always see flamingos at the lake?

Flamingos and water birds depend entirely on seasonal rainfall; during dry periods, the lakebed can be completely dry.

How do I get to Dayet Srji from Merzouga?

The lake is located a few kilometers from Merzouga village and is easily reached by 4x4, quad bike, or on a guided desert tour.
