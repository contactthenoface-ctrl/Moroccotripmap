Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Riad and a Dar?

A Riad traditionally features an interior garden or courtyard with trees, whereas a Dar is a smaller traditional house built around a central light well or tiled patio without large garden trees.

Is breakfast usually included in Moroccan Riads?

Yes! Almost all traditional riads offer a generous complimentary breakfast with fresh orange juice, Moroccan mint tea, msemen flatbreads, honey, jams, and eggs.
